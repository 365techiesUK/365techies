// HOW LONG IT TAKES

How long it takes, and why

Gmail accepts only about 500 MB a day of email copied into it this way. Go past that and it refuses more for up to a day — which is why moves done by hand so often stop part-way with an error. The app sends a little under the limit each day and then waits, so it never trips it.

First it copies your mail out of Virgin onto your PC, which takes from a few minutes to a few hours depending on the size. Then Gmail’s limit sets the pace:

Up to about 450 MBThe same day
1 GB2 to 3 days
3 GBAbout a week
5 GBAbout 12 days

The check tells you the exact size of your mailbox and the number of days before you start. Leave the PC switched on if you can. If you switch it off, the move carries on by itself the next time you switch it on, and it never sends the same email twice. Start well before the date in your email from Virgin.

Source: Google, Gmail bandwidth limits (500 MB a day uploaded over IMAP), read 29 September 2026.

// WHAT YOU NEED

What you need before you start

Nothing you have to buy. Most people have everything already, apart from the Google app password, which takes about five minutes to make.

Don’t have a Gmail account yet? Make one free with Google first, and use a password you don’t use anywhere else. Want to keep your Virgin address instead? That means signing up with Junara: what it costs.

  • A Windows 10 or 11 PC, desktop or laptop. It doesn’t run on a Mac.
  • Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge on it. Edge comes with Windows, so you almost certainly have it.
  • Your Virgin email address and its normal password, the one you use for Virgin webmail. Not an app password.
  • A Gmail account with 2-Step Verification on, and a Google app password for it.
  • Room on the PC for about as much again as your mailbox, because the mail is copied to your PC first. The check shows the size.
  • Time before your date: a few days for a big mailbox.

// STEP BY STEP

How to use it, step by step

  1. Download and install 365 PC Manager. It is free, with no sign-up. If Windows shows a blue ‘protected your PC’ box, click More info and check the publisher says 365 Techies Ltd.
  2. Choose Check my Virgin email. A browser window opens at Virgin’s webmail. Type your Virgin address on Virgin’s page, then its normal password. If Virgin asks you to register, or says it can’t sign you in just now, stop there and press ‘Stuck? We’ll do it for you’. Once you’re in, you see every folder, how many emails, the size, and how many days the move will take. Nothing is changed.
  3. Make a Google app password. On the Gmail account you are moving to: switch on 2-Step Verification, then make an app password.
  4. Choose Move it to Gmail myself. Type in your Gmail address and the app password, and start. It copies your mail to the PC, then sends it into Gmail, about 500 MB a day, carrying on by itself. Leave the PC on if you can.
  5. Check it arrived. When it finishes, the app counts each folder in Gmail against the original. Your folders are labels under ‘Virgin Media’.
  6. Change your address everywhere. Choose Where your address is used, change your address with each company, and set up forwarding on the Virgin mailbox while it still works.

Our guides for two of those steps: how to make a Google app password, and changing your email address everywhere afterwards.

Free, made by us. It works only on Virgin Media’s webmail, never changes anything in your mailbox, and sends nothing to us — the copy goes into your Downloads folder.

  4. Then choose Move it to Gmail myself, with your Gmail address and Google app password, as in the steps above.

// GOOD QUESTIONS

Frequently asked

How do I move my Virgin Media email to Gmail?

On a Windows PC: download our free 365 PC Manager app, choose Check my Virgin email and sign in to Virgin in the window it opens, make a Google app password for your Gmail, then choose Move it to Gmail myself. It copies every folder into Gmail, carries on by itself each day, and checks at the end that everything arrived. Or we do the whole move for you for £60 per email address, including a full service of your PC with a written report.

Is it free?

Yes. Checking your Virgin mailbox, moving it to Gmail and the list of where your address is used are all free in 365 PC Manager, with no sign-up and no card. We make it free because a lot of people are being asked to pay to keep an address they have had for years. If you would rather we did the whole move for you, that is £60 per email address, agreed before we start, and it includes a full service of your PC with a written report.

Do I need a Virgin app password?

No. When the app asks, a browser window opens at Virgin’s own webmail and you sign in there with your Virgin address and its normal password, the one you use for webmail. That is why it also works for a second mailbox on the account, which Virgin often won’t give an app password since the Virgin Media O2 ID change.

Why do I need a Google app password?

It is how Gmail lets a program on your PC put email into your account. You make it once in your Google Account, which needs 2-Step Verification switched on, and you can delete it when the move has finished. Our step-by-step guide shows you how.

How long does it take?

It depends on the size of your mailbox, because Gmail accepts only about 500 MB a day of email copied in this way, then refuses more for up to a day. So the app sends a little under that each day and carries on by itself. A few hundred MB goes across the same day; 3 GB takes about a week. The check tells you your own number before you start.

Will I lose any emails?

No. Nothing is deleted from Virgin: the app copies. At the end it counts every folder in Gmail against the original and shows you any difference. Spam and Deleted items are left out on purpose, and Gmail keeps only one copy of two identical emails, so a slightly lower count in Gmail can be normal.

Does it work for blueyonder, ntlworld and virgin.net addresses?

Yes. blueyonder.co.uk, ntlworld.com and virgin.net addresses all run on Virgin Media’s email system, with the same webmail, so the app treats them exactly like a virginmedia.com address.

Will it delete anything from my Virgin mailbox?

No. The check only reads, and the move copies. Nothing in your Virgin mailbox is moved, deleted or marked as read, so it all stays exactly where it is until you decide what to do with the Virgin address.

Will my folders and dates come across?

Yes. Each Virgin folder becomes a Gmail label under ‘Virgin Media’, and every email keeps its original date and whether you had read it. Spam and Deleted items are left out. At the end the app counts each folder in Gmail against the original, so you can see everything arrived.

Can it move a second Virgin mailbox?

Yes. When the Virgin sign-in window opens, sign in with that mailbox’s own address and password. The app moves one mailbox at a time, so do them one after the other.

Does it move my contacts and calendar?

No, it moves your email and folders. If you keep contacts in Virgin’s webmail and want them in Gmail too, ring us and we will help you bring them across.

Does it work on a Mac, iPad or phone?

No. 365 PC Manager is a Windows app, for Windows 10 and 11 desktops and laptops. Our £60 move is done by connecting to your PC, and our remote support covers Windows PCs only, so we can’t do it on a Mac, iPad or phone either.

Virgin says it can’t sign me in just now. What do I do?

That is Virgin’s own security check. Virgin now signs people in with the Virgin Media O2 ID, and it sometimes pauses a sign-in from a browser window it hasn’t seen before. Don’t keep trying, and don’t press Register: press ‘Stuck? We’ll do it for you’ in the app, or ring 01202 775566, and we move it for you — £60 per email address, agreed before we start, including a full service of your PC with a written report.

A new Chrome window says ‘Sign in to Chrome’. Should I?

No need: just ignore it. The app opens its own separate browser window for the Virgin sign-in, so it never touches your normal browser or its saved passwords, and it tidies that window away afterwards.

My mailbox has already moved to Junara. Will it still work?

The app reads Virgin Media’s own webmail. If you have already signed up and your mailbox has moved to Junara, ring us before you start and we will look at it with you. If you haven’t decided yet, see keep it or move it?

What does the app send to 365 Techies?

Never your emails, your passwords or your contacts: the move goes from Virgin to Gmail through your own PC. Like the rest of 365 PC Manager, it checks in about once an hour with your PC’s health basics, as described on the app’s page. If you press ‘Stuck? We’ll do it for you’, it sends us your name, your phone number and the size of your mailbox, so we can ring you back.
