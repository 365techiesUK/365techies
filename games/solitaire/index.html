    How to play

    1. The aim: build the four piles at the top, one for each suit, from Ace up to King.
    2. In the seven columns, put each card on one a step higher of the other colour — a red 6 on a black 7.
    3. Tap a card and it moves to the best place for it. You can drag cards too, if you prefer.
    4. Tap the deck at the top left to turn over new cards. When it’s empty, tap it to start again.
    5. Only a King can go in an empty column.
    6. Stuck? Press Hint and the next move lights up. Undo takes back as many moves as you like.

    Keys, if you like them: N new game, U undo, H hint, space turns the deck, F full screen.
