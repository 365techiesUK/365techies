// GOOD QUESTIONS

Frequently asked

Do you repair computers and laptops in West Moors?

Yes — we repair PCs and laptops for homes and businesses across West Moors and the surrounding area (Ferndown, Verwood, St Leonards and the surrounding area). We can help remotely, visit you at home, or collect the machine.

How much does a computer repair cost?

It depends on the problem — we diagnose it and give you a clear price before any work, with no call-out fee and no surprise bills. Many issues are fixed quickly and remotely.

Is it worth repairing or replacing my computer?

We’ll tell you honestly — sometimes a small upgrade like an SSD gives an old machine years more life; sometimes a replacement is better value. Try our repair or replace advisor.

Why is my computer so slow?

Usually clutter, too many start-up programs, low disk space, missed updates or a failing drive. We’ll find the cause and speed it up — and a monthly plan keeps it fast with a full service every six weeks.

Can you come to my home in West Moors?

Yes — we offer home visits across West Moors, and for any visit we phone ahead with an estimated arrival time so you know exactly when to expect us. We can also fix many things remotely.

Will I lose my files?

We take care to protect your data, and we can transfer or recover your files — we’ll always talk you through it first.

Do you do home visits in West Moors, or do I have to bring my computer to you?

We’re happy to come to you right across West Moors and the surrounding area — whether you’re near the village shops, out towards Ferndown, or on the quieter lanes leading up towards Moors Valley and Verwood. Lots of our customers, especially those who are retired or simply prefer not to unplug everything, find a home visit far easier. Just give us a ring on 01202 775566 and we’ll arrange a time that suits you.
