Seafront

A free browser game on the real Bournemouth seafront — the pier, the beach, Boscombe, and the Dorset coast round to Old Harry Rocks. No download, no sign-up.

Play now →

Opens straight into the game. It takes a moment to load the first time.

Eight ways to play

  • Free ride — open water, nothing chasing you.
  • Rescue mode — swimmers in trouble off the beach.
  • Viking raid — longships at the pier. Save it.
  • Pirate raid — the same siege, a corsair fleet.
  • The Harbour Mouth — Old Harry Rocks.
  • The Smuggling Run — the Dorset coast at night.
  • The Stunt Stage — a ramp course off Boscombe.
  • The Trip Back — a boatload too many in a swell.

Controls

  • Mouse, or AD to steer
  • Scroll, or ↑↓ for throttle
  • WS to trim · Space to punch it
  • C camera · K craft · L levels
  • An Xbox controller works if you have one plugged in
  • On a phone or tablet, it is all touch

You will need

  • A browser with WebGL2 — anything current
  • It runs best on a desktop or a recent phone

Three craft

An electric hydrofoil, a jet ski and a rigid inflatable, each with its own handling. The sea is simulated rather than animated, so the swell you are riding is the swell the boat is reacting to, and the coast is built from survey data rather than drawn by eye.
