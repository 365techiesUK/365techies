    // HOW IT WORKS

    How to work out your managed IT support cost per computer

    365 Techies’ managed IT support is priced per computer — home support is £18.25/month per computer and business support from £24.38/month per computer, with optional Microsoft 365 at £4.85 per user — and this calculator adds them up live as you set the number of computers and licences.

    Step by step

    1. Choose home or business — Pick the Home & Family or Business tab at the top of the calculator — each uses its own per-computer price.
    2. Set your computers — Slide to the number of computers or laptops you need supported (home goes 1–10, business 1–25). The monthly total updates instantly.
    3. Add Microsoft 365 if needed — Use the + and − buttons to add a Microsoft 365 licence (£4.85/user per month) for each person who needs one — leave it at zero if not.
    4. Read your monthly total — The summary panel shows a line-by-line breakdown and a clear per-month total — business figures show as a ‘from’ guide because larger teams are tailored.
    5. Confirm the exact figure — Every plan is cancel-anytime and includes a full computer service every six weeks; tap through to view plans or talk to us and we’ll confirm your exact price before anything starts.

    Good to know

    How much is managed IT support per computer in the UK?

    With 365 Techies it is £18.25 per month per computer for home support and from £24.38 per month per computer for business support, with Microsoft 365 an optional £4.85 per user per month. The calculator multiplies these by the number of computers you choose so you see a real monthly total, not a generic estimate.

    Does the cost calculator give an exact quote?

    It gives accurate guide prices taken straight from our live per-computer plans, so it is what you would expect to pay. Business totals show as a ‘from’ figure because larger teams are tailored to how you work (email, hosting, users); we always confirm the exact figure before anything starts, with no hidden call-out fees.

    Is there a minimum contract for per-computer IT support?

    No. Every 365 Techies plan is billed monthly and is cancel-anytime, so you pay per computer per month with no long lock-in and can add or remove computers and Microsoft 365 licences as your home or business changes.
