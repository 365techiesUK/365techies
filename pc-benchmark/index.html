// GOOD QUESTIONS

Frequently asked

How does a browser benchmark work?

It runs six real workloads in your browser — heavy maths on one core and then across all of them (using web workers), hardware-accelerated SHA-256 encryption, large memory copies, a WebGL graphics stress test and browser storage reads and writes — each with a live readout as it runs. It’s indicative rather than lab-grade, but it gives an honest picture of how your machine is performing.

Can I share or keep my score?

Yes — download your score card as an image to share or keep, or copy your results as text. Your browser also remembers your previous run, so when you benchmark again (say, after a tune-up or an SSD upgrade) you’ll see exactly how many points you gained.

Is it safe? Will it harm my computer?

Completely safe — it simply makes your computer work hard for about 20 seconds, like opening a big spreadsheet or playing a game. The fan may spin up briefly; nothing is installed, sent or stored.

Why is my score different from other benchmark tools?

Every benchmark uses its own scale, and ours is a 0–100 scale of our own — so don’t compare the number with native suites like Geekbench or Cinebench. Compare your machine against our bands, or re-run it after a tune-up to see the difference.

My score is low — what should I do?

Often a machine benches low for fixable reasons: an old-style hard drive (an SSD upgrade transforms it), too little memory, or years of accumulated clutter. We’ll diagnose it honestly — book a tune-up, or see if it’s time for a repair or replacement.

Does it work on phones and Macs?

Yes — it runs on any modern device, though the scoring is tuned with Windows PCs in mind (we’re Windows specialists). It’s a fun way to compare your phone with your laptop!

The graphics test says ‘not available’ — why?

Your browser has WebGL disabled or blocked (some strict privacy setups do this). The rest of the benchmark still runs and your overall score simply skips graphics.

// HOW IT WORKS

How to test your computer’s speed online

This free benchmark runs six real tests of your processor (on one core and all cores), encryption, memory, graphics and storage right in your browser and gives a single speed score out of 100 — no download and nothing installed.

Step by step

  1. Close other programs — Shut anything heavy so the test measures your computer, not what else is running.
  2. Press Start — The benchmark runs six short tests live — processor, encryption, memory, graphics and storage — in about 20 seconds.
  3. Read your score — You get a clear score out of 100 with a band from ‘struggling’ to ‘blazing fast’, plus a per-test breakdown.
  4. Save or compare it — Download your score card, and re-run it after a tune-up or an SSD upgrade to see the jump.

Good to know

How can I test my computer’s speed online for free?

Just run this benchmark in your browser — it puts your processor, memory, graphics and storage through six real tests and gives a speed score out of 100 in about 20 seconds. Nothing is installed and nothing is sent to us.

Is an online speed test as accurate as installed software?

It’s indicative rather than lab-grade — browser and background apps affect it — but it’s a genuinely useful, honest picture of how your machine is performing, and it’s perfect for spotting a slow disk or comparing before and after an upgrade.
