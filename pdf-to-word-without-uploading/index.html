// PDF TO WORD, ON YOUR OWN PC

Turn a PDF into Word, without uploading it

You need to change a letter, a form or a CV that you only have as a PDF. Search for “PDF to Word” and every answer wants you to upload it to a website first, which isn’t great for a bank letter or anything from the doctor. Here are the free ways to do it on your own Windows PC, where the file never leaves it. Rated 4.9 on Google.

/01 — START HERE

Which way is right for you

  • You have Microsoft Word (it comes with Microsoft 365): open the PDF in Word. It keeps the layout best (next section).
  • You don’t have Word: our free Techies PDF puts the PDF’s words into a Word document you can change. It’s the words only, not the layout (section 3).
  • It’s a scan or a photo of a letter: the words are a picture, so something has to read them first (section 4).
  • You only need to sign it, fill it in or fix one date: you may not need Word at all (section 6).

Whichever you choose, your original PDF is kept. You get a new Word file alongside it.

/02 — IF YOU HAVE WORD

Open the PDF in Microsoft Word

  1. Open Word.
  2. Choose File, then Open, and pick the PDF.
  3. Word says it will turn the PDF into a Word document. Choose OK.
  4. Make your changes, then File, Save as, and save it as a Word document, or as a PDF again.

Microsoft explains what happens: Word makes a copy of the PDF, converts it and tries to match the original layout, and your original PDF is kept. It works best with documents that are mostly words. A page that is mostly charts or pictures may come in as one picture, and some things are known to come out wrong, including tables with spaced-out cells, page borders and colours, footnotes that run over more than one page, and comments (Microsoft). Check it before you send it.

Microsoft lists this for Word for Microsoft 365, Word 2024, 2021, 2019 and 2016. Word on your PC does the job itself: you don’t upload the PDF to a website.

/03 — NO WORD? USE OUR FREE APP

No Word? Get the words into a Word document with Techies PDF

We made Techies PDF for the PDF jobs our customers ring us about, and it’s free. It runs on your Windows PC and doesn’t send your files anywhere. Get it from the Microsoft Store, where Microsoft checked it before listing it, or straight from us (below).

  1. Open Techies PDF and press Turn a PDF into a Word document.
  2. Choose the PDF.
  3. Press Save as a Word document and choose where to save it.

What you get: the PDF’s words, a line in Word for each line of the PDF and a new page for each page. The layout, pictures and fonts aren’t kept, and the program tells you so. It’s right for getting the words of a letter back so you can change them, rather than for copying a glossy leaflet.

The file opens in Microsoft Word, in our free Techies One, or in any program that opens Word documents.

/04 — A SCANNED LETTER

When the PDF is a scan

If you can’t select the words in the PDF with your mouse, it’s probably a scan or a photo of the page. The words are a picture, so there’s nothing to put in Word until something reads them.

In Techies PDF, when the pages are pictures, press Read the words in the scanned pages. Windows’ own text reader reads them on your computer, so the letter is never sent anywhere. Then save them as a Word document as above.

Two honest warnings. Windows reads scans well but not perfectly, so check names, numbers and dates afterwards. And a few PCs don’t have Windows’ text reader yet: the program offers to add it.

/05 — YOUR PRIVACY

Why not just use a converter website?

The free “PDF to Word” websites mostly work. But every one of them means uploading your file to someone else’s computer, and the PDFs people want to change are often the private ones: bank and pension letters, medical letters, solicitors’ letters, a CV with your address and phone number, a scan of an ID.

The same goes for putting it in Google Drive and opening it with Google Docs: it works, but the file is uploaded to Google first.

For anything private, read the website’s privacy policy before you upload it, or do it on your own PC instead, where it never leaves.

/06 — YOU MAY NOT NEED WORD

Only need to sign it, fill it in or fix one thing?

Turning a PDF into Word is the long way round for a lot of jobs. Techies PDF does these to the PDF itself, on your PC:

  • Fill in a form and sign it, and save it ready to send back.
  • Change a word, a date or a name: cover the old words and type the new ones, saved as a new copy.
  • Join PDFs together, take pages out, or turn upside-down pages the right way up.
  • Make a PDF smaller so it fits in an email.

// GOOD QUESTIONS

Frequently asked

How do I convert a PDF to Word for free?

If you have Microsoft Word, open the PDF in Word with File, then Open: it converts it for you. If you don’t, our free Techies PDF program for Windows puts a PDF’s words into a Word document on your PC, without uploading it.

Can I convert a PDF to Word without uploading it?

Yes. Word on your PC converts it itself, and Techies PDF does the job on your computer too. The converter websites and Google Docs both need the file uploaded first.

Will the Word document look the same as the PDF?

Not always. Microsoft says Word tries to match the layout but some things, such as some tables, page borders and footnotes, can come out differently, and a page of charts may come in as a picture. Techies PDF keeps the words only, not the layout, pictures or fonts.

How do I turn a scanned PDF into Word?

A scan is a picture of the words, so they have to be read first. In Techies PDF, press Read the words in the scanned pages: Windows’ own text reader reads them on your PC, then you can save them as a Word document. Check names, numbers and dates afterwards.

Are online PDF to Word converters safe?

Many work, but they all mean uploading your document to someone else’s computer. For bank, medical or legal letters, read the site’s privacy policy first, or convert it on your own PC instead.

Does it work on a Mac or an iPad?

Techies PDF is for Windows 10 and 11, and we help with Windows PCs only, not Macs, iPads or iPhones.

Still stuck with a PDF?

We sort it on your Windows PC while you watch. We work on Windows PCs only, not Macs. Call 01202 775566 or text 07520 615332.
