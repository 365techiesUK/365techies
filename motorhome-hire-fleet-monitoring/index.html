// HIRE-FLEET BATTERY MONITORING

Every Hire Motorhome’s Battery on One Live Screen

You hand over the keys; the last thing you need is a call from a lay-by because the leisure battery is flat. A custom VRM dashboard puts every van’s state of charge, solar and alerts on one hosted screen — built, hosted and supported for you, worldwide. Rated 4.9 on Google.

/01 — THE ONE-SCREEN ANSWER

Every van’s battery, solar and charge state on one screen

Motorhome hire fleet battery monitoring means seeing the state of charge, solar input and charge status of every vehicle you run — live, on a single screen — instead of logging into each van one at a time. When each motorhome carries a Victron GX device, its data already flows to Victron’s VRM portal; we build a custom dashboard on top of that data through the official VRM API so your whole fleet reads at a glance.

One hosted screen shows every van as a tile: state of charge (SOC), solar yield, whether it’s charging or discharging, and a clear flag the moment a leisure battery drifts low. It runs full-screen on the office TV for the whole team to see, and reflows to the duty manager’s phone out of hours. No spreadsheet, no per-van login, no guesswork about which vehicle needs attention before it goes out on hire.

The one hard prerequisite is Victron kit in each van — a GX device such as a Cerbo GX, plus a way to get online. We’re honest about that up front, and we or your installer can spec it. Everything after that is done for you: we build it, host it and support it, so your turnaround team gets a screen, not a server to look after.

/02 — WHY IT PAYS

Catch the flat battery before the renter does

The whole point of fleet monitoring is one outcome: fewer breakdown callouts. A leisure battery that has quietly sulked down to 20% on the forecourt becomes a phone call from a lay-by two hours later — a recovery van, a refund conversation, and a one-star review about a holiday that started in the dark.

Seeing SOC across the fleet turns that into a five-minute job before handover. The turnaround team spots the low tile, puts the van on charge or swaps it, and the renter never knows there was a problem. Over a season that is the difference between chasing faults reactively and running a fleet you can trust.

  • Pre-handover check: confirm every booked van is charged and healthy before the keys go out, from one screen.
  • Fewer callouts: a flat or failing battery caught on the forecourt costs minutes, not a recovery and a refund.
  • Better reviews: holidays that start smoothly end in five-star ratings; power problems do the opposite.
  • Kinder to the batteries: spotting vans left deeply discharged between hires helps your lithium or AGM banks last their full life.

None of this replaces your walk-round. It just means walking up to the right van already knowing what you’ll find.

/03 — WHERE VRM STOPS

VRM is excellent — here’s where a hire desk outgrows it

Let’s give Victron’s VRM portal its due: it is genuinely excellent, free, and rock-solid at what it does. Each van logs to VRM, you get history, alarms and remote diagnostics, and Victron even added a Fleet Dashboard that gives installers an at-a-glance view of alarms and key data across many sites, with a map for GPS-equipped vehicles. For plenty of operators that is genuinely enough — and if it is, we’ll happily tell you so rather than sell you a screen you don’t need.

Where a hire business tends to outgrow stock VRM is the last mile:

  • The Fleet Dashboard only appears once your account is linked to 10 or more sites, and it is Victron’s layout, not yours — you can’t rebrand it, restyle it to your hire-desk workflow, or put it on a reception TV as your own screen.
  • It lives behind a VRM login. That’s right for a manager triaging systems, but awkward for a front-desk TV or a duty phone that non-technical staff need to read at a glance.
  • The stock main dashboard layout is fixed; only the Advanced tab is composable, and custom widgets are limited to roughly six parameters.
  • VRM share links and iframe embeds are read-only and refresh at the logging interval (15 minutes by default) — not the live, branded screen you want on the wall.

To be clear: we are an independent service and are listed in Victron Energy’s Recommended Software Integrator Program — though not a Victron dealer, distributor or reseller. Victron and VRM are their trademarks. Our dashboards build on VRM through its official API — they never replace it.

/04 — THE SCREEN WE BUILD

A branded fleet screen tuned to your turnaround

We take the live data VRM already holds for your vans and build the screen you actually want on the wall: your fleet, your branding, your rules.

  • Every van at a glance: one tile per vehicle showing SOC, solar and charging state, sorted so anything low floats to the top.
  • Plain-English alerts: thresholds tuned to your turnaround — for example, flag any booked van under a set SOC the evening before collection — so the right vehicle gets attention first.
  • Where are my vans: for vehicles with a GPS-enabled GX device, a map view so you can see location alongside power state.
  • Reads anywhere: full-screen on the office TV without anyone logging in, and reflowed cleanly onto the duty manager’s phone out of hours.
  • Your look: your colours, logo and vehicle names — not registration hashes and stock widgets.

Because we build on the official VRM API rather than dropping in an iframe, the screen isn’t locked to a stock share link’s refresh — which only updates at the logging interval — and we can lay it out for people who don’t know a MultiPlus from a mains lead. If you’d rather your data landed in Grafana, or embedded in your own booking portal, we do that too — the underlying approach is the same, only the destination changes.

/05 — SEE IT LIVE

This isn’t a mock-up — watch our own van stream

We don’t ask you to take this on faith. Our own off-grid support van — the 365 Crafter, running a Cerbo GX, Venus OS, MPPT solar and a lithium bank — streams live through the official VRM API on our dashboards page, right now.

On our live custom VRM dashboards you can see the same van rendered in two completely different dashboard styles, which proves the data is real and the layout is ours to shape. There is also a fleet-style demo showing how a multi-vehicle screen behaves — and we label it honestly: only the Crafter is a real, live system; the other vehicles are illustrative placeholders so you can picture your own fleet in the grid.

That is the exact engine behind a hire-fleet screen: real Victron hardware, real VRM data, our layout on top. When you look at your quote, you’ll already have seen the product working rather than a slide of one. Scale the grid from one van to a hundred and the principle doesn’t change.

/06 — WHAT EACH VAN NEEDS

The one prerequisite: a GX device in each vehicle

Here is the honest constraint. A dashboard can only show data that exists, and VRM only receives data from a Victron GX device. So each vehicle needs a GX device — commonly a Cerbo GX — wired to its Victron charger/inverter and battery monitor, plus a way to get online (a GX LTE/4G module with a SIM, or the vehicle’s own connectivity).

That gives you the SOC, solar and charge data per van that the fleet screen relies on. Non-Victron sensors can be brought in too, but only where they can be fed through the GX device — we’ll tell you plainly what will and won’t appear.

If your fleet is already Victron-equipped, we can usually start with nothing more than VRM access. If it isn’t, that’s a conversation before the dashboard, not a nasty surprise after it: we can specify the GX and connectivity for each van, or work alongside the installer who fits your conversions. See our Victron system builder and off-grid Victron energy pages for the hardware side.

The same applies beyond motorhomes: a boat-charter or day-hire fleet with Victron systems monitors identically — one GX device per vessel, one screen for the marina office.

/07 — WHAT IT COSTS

Quote-based, and it scales per vehicle

We don’t publish a fixed price because a two-van boutique operator and a fifty-van national aren’t the same job — but we’re happy to be transparent about what drives the number so there are no surprises.

  • Fleet size: the design is largely a one-off, but a per-vehicle element covers each van’s tile, alerts and ongoing data handling — so the quote scales with how many vehicles you run.
  • How custom: a clean branded fleet grid is one thing; bespoke workflows, a booking-portal embed, a Grafana build or map/geofence features add design time.
  • Hosting & support: we build, host and maintain the screen so your team never touches a server — that runs as an ongoing service.
  • Your starting point: a fully Victron-equipped fleet is quicker to onboard than one that still needs GX devices specified and fitted.

It’s a done-for-you service delivered remotely, worldwide. We’re a family-run UK firm (Bournemouth) that has been trading since 1995, so response times are quoted honestly against UK office hours, Monday to Friday, 9–5 UK time. Tell us how many vans you run and what’s fitted, and we’ll come back with a live demo tailored to you and a clear quote. Start on our contact page or call +44 1202 775566.

// GOOD QUESTIONS

Frequently asked

Do our motorhomes need Victron kit already?

Yes — each van needs a Victron GX device (typically a Cerbo GX) feeding its charger, inverter and battery monitor, plus a way to get online. That’s what VRM logs and what our dashboard reads. If your fleet is already Victron-equipped, we often just need VRM access to begin. If not, we or your installer can specify the kit per vehicle first — we’ll always tell you honestly what’s needed before quoting.

How is this different from VRM’s own Fleet Dashboard?

VRM’s Fleet Dashboard is genuinely good, and free, but it needs 10 or more linked sites, it’s Victron’s fixed layout rather than yours, and it sits behind a VRM login. We build a screen in your branding, tuned to your hire-desk workflow, that runs on the office TV without anyone logging in. We’re independent — not affiliated with or endorsed by Victron — and we build on VRM’s official API, never replacing it.

Can the screen alert us before a battery goes flat?

Yes. VRM already supports alarm rules on parameters like state of charge, with email and app push notifications. On top of that, our dashboard flags low vans visually and can be tuned to your turnaround — for example, highlighting any booked vehicle under a set SOC the evening before collection — so the right van gets charged or swapped before the keys go out, not after the breakdown call.

Will it show where each van is?

If a van’s GX device has GPS, yes — VRM records location and we can show a map view alongside each vehicle’s power state, so you can see which vans are out, which are back, and their battery health together. Location depends on that GPS hardware being fitted; where a vehicle has no GPS module you still get full battery, solar and charge data, just without the map pin.

Can it go on our reception TV without a login?

That’s a big reason operators come to us. VRM’s own views need a login, which is awkward for a shared TV. Because we render through the official VRM API, we can run a full-screen, branded fleet view (see our TV kiosk display service) that sits on the reception or workshop TV around the clock, and reflows onto the duty manager’s phone out of hours — no one signing in, no stock widgets.

How do we get started, and what does it cost?

Tell us how many vans you run and what Victron kit is fitted, via our contact page or on 01202 775566. We’ll show you the live demo on this site, confirm what your fleet needs, and send a clear, quote-based price that scales per vehicle — no invented tiers and no obligation. It’s a done-for-you service: we build, host and support the screen so your team never touches a server.

How live is the data really?

More responsive than a stock VRM share link, but let’s be precise. Share links and iframe embeds are read-only and refresh only at the logging interval — 15 minutes by default. Because we build on the official VRM API, our fleet screen isn’t tied to that fixed interval and can refresh more often, which matters when you’re checking a van minutes before handover. It’s monitoring rather than control, and not sub-second telemetry: the dashboard shows state and alerts, it doesn’t switch your kit on or off.

Do you work with our installer, and outside the UK?

Both. We deliver everything remotely and support Victron fleets worldwide, so your location isn’t a barrier. We’re glad to work alongside the installer who builds your conversions — they handle the vehicles, we handle the dashboard — or to specify the GX and connectivity ourselves. As a UK firm, we quote response times honestly against office hours, Monday to Friday, 9–5 UK time.

We also run boat hire — can you cover that?

Yes. A boat-charter or day-hire fleet running Victron systems monitors exactly like a motorhome fleet — one GX device per vessel logging to VRM, one branded screen for the marina office. The same is true for glamping bell tents, holiday lets and event power. If your assets carry Victron kit, we can put them on a single hosted dashboard, wherever they are.

Can the fleet screen live on our own booking website?

It can — something like fleet.yourhirecompany.co.uk, one click away from your booking pages, so staff bookmark your address rather than ours. Your VRM token stays in a single file on your server that visitors can never see in the page code, and the SFTP access you give us covers only that subdomain’s folder — the rest of your site stays yours alone. As the fleet grows, we simply add vans, screens and features in the same place.
