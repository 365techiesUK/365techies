/01 — THE CALLS YOU NEVER HEAR ABOUT

The calls you never hear about

Most small businesses lose enquiries the same way. The phone rings at 6.40pm and goes to voicemail. It rings at 11am while everyone is with a customer, and the caller waits four rings, hangs up and dials the next company on the list. Very few people leave a voicemail any more — and fewer still wait for a call back.

You rarely see this happen, because a missed call leaves almost no trace. There is no enquiry form in your inbox, no note in your diary, no name to ring back. Just a number in a call log that nobody checks.

An AI voice agent closes that gap. It answers when you can’t — out of hours, on overflow (when every line is busy), or whenever you route calls to it — has a natural conversation with the caller, and makes sure the enquiry ends up somewhere your team will actually see it.

/02 — A CONNECTED ENQUIRY WORKFLOW, NOT JUST A RECEPTIONIST

A connected enquiry workflow, not just a receptionist

Plenty of services will answer your phone. The difference here is what happens after the call ends.

We design your voice agent as a complete enquiry workflow. The conversation is only the first half; the second half is deterministic automation — ordinary, reliable software logic — that logs the enquiry, raises a ticket or CRM record, and notifies the right person by SMS, email or Slack. AI does the one step where it genuinely helps: understanding a caller who speaks naturally. Everything downstream runs on fixed rules, because a follow-up should never depend on an AI making a judgement call.

That split matters. It means the agent can be flexible in conversation but completely predictable in what it does with the information — every call handled the same way, every enquiry landing in the same place. It’s the same approach we use across our AI agents work: AI where it helps, plain automation everywhere else.

/03 — WHAT THE VOICE AGENT HANDLES

What the voice agent handles

Each agent is designed around your business, but the building blocks are consistent:

  • After-hours and overflow answering

    calls route to the agent when you’re closed, engaged or simply busy, on rules you choose.

  • Enquiry capture

    the caller’s name, number and what they need, taken in a natural conversation rather than a rigid menu.

  • Answering agreed questions

    opening hours, coverage area, how your service works — drawn only from information you have approved. The agent doesn’t improvise answers.

  • Booking and callback capture

    the agent can take a booking request or arrange a callback slot, so the caller leaves with a next step rather than a promise.

  • Message taking

    a clear, structured message instead of a mumbled voicemail.

  • Human transfer

    during staffed hours, callers who need a person are put through to one. The agent is an extra layer, not a wall.

  • The follow-up workflow

    every call generates a record in your CRM or ticketing system and a notification to your team, automatically.

/04 — HOW A CALL FLOWS, STEP BY STEP

How a call flows, step by step

Here is a typical flow — yours is designed with you, but this is the shape of it:

  1. 1

    1. The call routes to the agent

    out of hours, after a set number of rings, or whenever your line is engaged. Your existing number stays the front door.

  2. 2

    2. The caller is told it’s an AI

    a clear, friendly disclosure at the start of every call. We don’t build agents that pretend to be human.

  3. 3

    3. The agent asks what they need

    and the caller answers in their own words. No “press one for sales”.

  4. 4

    4. It answers or captures

    agreed questions get answered from your approved information; anything else becomes a structured enquiry with name, number and details.

  5. 5

    5. It offers a next step

    a booking request, a callback slot, or a transfer to your team if someone is available and the caller needs a person now.

  6. 6

    6. The call ends and is recorded to your agreed setup

    recording and transcription are configured with you, with retention you have signed off, not defaults you never saw.

  7. 7

    7. The follow-up fires

    the enquiry is written into your CRM or raised as a ticket, and your team is notified by SMS, email or Slack. By the time you pick up your phone, the enquiry is already in your system with a record of what was said.

Nothing in steps six and seven involves AI judgement — it’s fixed automation, which is exactly why it’s dependable.

/05 — HONEST ABOUT THE DETAILS

Honest about the details

A few things other providers tend to gloss over, stated plainly:

  • Callers always know.

    The agent discloses that it’s an AI assistant at the start of every call. In our experience people are fine with that — what frustrates callers is not being answered at all.

  • Recording and data are agreed, not assumed.

    Running the service means calls are processed, and usually recorded and transcribed, by the underlying telephony and AI platforms. We document exactly what is stored, where, and for how long, and we set retention with you — the setup is designed to support your obligations under UK data protection law, and you’ll see it in writing before anything goes live.

  • Usage costs are itemised.

    Call minutes and telephony charges depend on your call volume, so we identify them line by line in your quote rather than burying them in the subscription or promising they’re “included forever”.

  • Humans stay in control.

    The agent captures and routes; it doesn’t commit your business to anything. Anything consequential goes to a person, and transfer to your team is part of the design, not an afterthought.

/07 — WHAT IT COSTS

What it costs

The pricing follows the same model as every service on our AI & automation pages: a monthly service subscription plus a one-off design and build.

  • Service subscription — from £95/month.

    This covers running the agent, monitoring, support from our team, and ongoing adjustments to what it says and where enquiries go.

  • Design and build — one-off, quoted by complexity.

    A simple after-hours answering and message flow sits at one end; an agent wired into your CRM, booking system and ticketing sits at the other. The quote reflects the number of call flows, the questions you want answered, and the systems we connect.

  • Telephony and usage — identified in your quote.

    Call minutes vary by business, so we show them separately and honestly.

// GOOD QUESTIONS

Frequently asked

How much does an AI voice agent cost?

The service subscription starts from £95/month, which covers running the agent plus monitoring, support and ongoing adjustments by our team. The design and build is a separate one-off cost, quoted by complexity — a simple message-taking flow costs less to build than an agent connected to your CRM and booking system. Call minutes and telephony charges are identified separately in your quote, so you see the full running cost up front.

Will callers know they are talking to an AI?

Yes, always. The agent discloses at the start of every call that it’s an AI assistant. We don’t build agents that pretend to be human — and in practice, callers care far more about being answered and helped than about who answered.

What happens if the agent can't help a caller?

During staffed hours it transfers the caller to your team. Out of hours it takes a structured message, offers a callback slot, and notifies you immediately — so the caller always leaves with a next step, and you always know the call happened. The agent only answers questions from information you have approved; it never guesses.

Will this replace our receptionist?

That’s not how we design it. The agent handles the calls a person can’t — after hours, when the line is engaged, when everyone is with a customer. If you have someone answering the phone, they keep doing what they do well, and the agent stops enquiries falling through the gaps around them.

Do we need a new phone number or new lines?

Usually not. Your existing number stays the front door, and calls divert to the agent on rules you choose — out of hours, after a set number of rings, or on overflow. We handle the telephony setup as part of the build, and any line or call costs are identified in your quote.

What happens to call recordings and caller data?

We tell you exactly what is stored, where, and for how long, before anything goes live. Recording and transcription settings and retention periods are agreed with you, and the setup is designed to support your obligations under UK data protection law. Every enquiry the agent captures lands in your systems — your CRM, your ticketing, your notifications — not in a black box.
