If your email is Gmail, it never stopped

Outlook is a program for reading email. Your email itself lives with whoever gave you the address. If that is Google, Yahoo, BT, Sky or Virgin Media, a Microsoft outage doesn’t touch it. What stops is the way in: the new Outlook reads Gmail and Yahoo accounts through Microsoft’s own servers, so when those have a bad day your inbox can go quiet too.

If Microsoft is down: what to do

  1. Leave your settings alone. Don’t change your password, remove your account or reinstall Outlook. None of that helps while the fault is at Microsoft’s end, and it can leave you with a second problem once the service is back.
  2. Work out where your email lives. Look at the address after the @ sign.
    • gmail.com, yahoo.co.uk, btinternet.com, sky.com, virginmedia.com and the like: your email is with that provider and is still arriving. Read it on the provider’s own website until Outlook is back: mail.google.com for Gmail, mail.yahoo.com for Yahoo, and your provider’s own sign-in page for the rest.
    • outlook.com, hotmail.com, live.co.uk, msn.com: your email is with Microsoft. No program, phone app or website can show it until Microsoft recovers.
    • A work or business address: if your firm uses Microsoft 365, it is with Microsoft too. Your IT person can see Microsoft’s detailed notices in the Microsoft 365 admin centre.
  3. Don’t expect the phone app to rescue you. Outlook on a phone talks to the same service. If the service is down, the app is down.
  4. For anything urgent, ring or text. Tell the people who matter that your email is slow today.
  5. Your emails are very unlikely to be lost. When a mail service can’t accept a message, the sender’s system keeps trying, usually for several days. Expect a backlog to arrive in a rush once things recover, possibly out of order.

If Microsoft says all is well: it is probably just you

Microsoft’s public feed shows widespread problems only. If it reads clear and Outlook still won’t behave, start with the quickest test: open outlook.office.com (work) or outlook.live.com (personal) in your browser and sign in. If your email is there, your account is fine and the fault is the program on this PC. Then pick the page that matches what you see:

The outage of 12 and 13 November 2026

On Thursday 12 November 2026, from around midday US Eastern time, Outlook, Teams, SharePoint, OneDrive and Copilot began failing for Microsoft 365 users around the world. Problems ran into Friday 13 November, at least 18 hours for some people, before Microsoft reported that email delivery was returning to normal.

Microsoft’s explanation was that the part of its servers that handles secure connections became overwhelmed and stopped processing requests. It said it had found no malicious activity behind the outage. It applied a configuration change and restarted the affected servers to recover.

GOOD QUESTIONS

Frequently asked

Is Outlook down right now?

The check at the top of this page reads Microsoft’s own status feed and tests Outlook on the web from your connection, so it answers for this minute. Microsoft’s public feed lists widespread incidents only: a fault with one account or one organisation won’t appear there.

Will I lose emails sent to me while Outlook is down?

It is very unlikely. When a mail service can’t accept a message, the sender’s system keeps trying, usually for several days. Messages sent to you during an outage normally arrive once the service recovers, sometimes late and out of order.

Can I still read my Gmail if Outlook is down?

Yes. Gmail is Google’s service, so sign in at mail.google.com. The new Outlook reads Gmail and Yahoo accounts through Microsoft’s servers, which is why a Microsoft outage can stop them appearing in Outlook even though Gmail itself is working.

Does Outlook on my phone work when Outlook is down?

Not if the fault is the Microsoft service. The phone app, the program on your PC and Outlook on the web all talk to the same servers. If only the program on your PC is broken, the phone app and the website will still work, which is a quick way to tell the two apart.

How long do Microsoft outages usually last?

Most are over within a few hours. The outage that began on 12 November 2026 was unusually long, lasting at least 18 hours for some users before Microsoft reported that service was returning to normal.

Was the November 2026 outage a hack?

Microsoft said it had found no malicious activity. Its explanation was that the part of its servers handling secure connections became overwhelmed and stopped processing requests.
