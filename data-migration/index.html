Data Migration Services

Data migration done safely

Moving data to a new computer, off a failing drive, or between storage and Microsoft 365? We transfer your files, photos, email and settings safely and in full — nothing lost, nothing left behind. Bournemouth, Poole, Christchurch and across Dorset. Rated 4.9 on Google.

  • ● Bournemouth, Poole & Dorset

Data migration is the careful business of getting everything you care about from one place to another — old laptop to new, a dying hard drive to a healthy one, an external disk into OneDrive, or one Microsoft 365 mailbox into another — without losing a single file along the way. It sounds simple. It rarely is. Photos hide in five different folders, email lives half on the device and half in the cloud, browser bookmarks and saved passwords vanish if you forget them, and a drive that's already struggling can fail completely the moment you start copying from it.

That's exactly when a careful pair of hands pays for itself. We've moved data for hundreds of local homes and small businesses since 1995, and we treat every migration the same way: take stock of what exists, copy it safely, then check — file by file — that it actually arrived. You get your documents, pictures, music, email, contacts and settings on the new device exactly as they were, with a plain-English explanation of where everything went. We support Windows and Android; we don't work on Apple Mac, iPhone or iPad.

// WHAT WE COVER

Data Migration that just works

Old PC to new PC

Files, photos, music, email, contacts, bookmarks and settings transferred from your old Windows computer to the new one, set up the way you're used to.

Off a failing drive

If your hard drive is making noises, running painfully slowly or throwing errors, we get your data off it carefully before it gives up entirely — speed matters here.

Into OneDrive & SharePoint

Move files and photos off loose USB sticks and external drives into Microsoft 365 so they're backed up, synced and reachable from any device.

Email & mailbox moves

Migrate email, contacts and calendars between accounts or into Microsoft 365 — old messages and folders preserved, nothing orphaned.

Phone & tablet data

Photos, contacts and files moved to or from Android phones and tablets, and synced so your devices stay in step.

Checked, not just copied

We verify the transfer against the original — counts, key folders and a spot-check together — so you can be certain everything made it across.

// HOW IT WORKS

Simple, honest, sorted

  1. 01

    We take stock first

    Before touching anything we look at what you've got and where it lives — documents, photos, email, software logins, settings — and agree exactly what needs to move and where it's going.

  2. 02

    We copy it safely

    We transfer your data to the new device, drive or cloud account using the safest route for your situation. With a failing drive we work gently and prioritise, so the most important data comes off first.

  3. 03

    We check, then hand over

    We confirm everything arrived — together where it helps — set the new device up the way you like it, and only retire or wipe the old one once you're happy nothing's missing.

// GOOD QUESTIONS

Frequently asked

Will I lose anything during the migration?

That's the whole point of doing it carefully — no. We take stock of what exists before we start, copy everything across, then check the result against the original together so you can see nothing's missing. We don't wipe or retire the old device until you've confirmed you're happy. If a drive is already failing we can't always guarantee 100% of a dying disk, but we get the most important data off first and are honest with you about what we find.

My old computer is broken or won't start — can you still get my data?

Often, yes. If the computer won't boot but the hard drive itself is intact, we can usually connect the drive separately and pull your files off it. If the drive itself is the problem, the sooner you stop using it the better — every extra power-on risks more damage. Bring it in or book a visit and we'll assess it honestly before doing anything.

How long does a data migration take?

It depends on how much data you have and how it's stored. A typical home computer with documents, photos and email can often be done in a single session; large photo libraries, slow external drives or business mailboxes take longer. We'll give you a realistic estimate once we've seen what's involved — no surprises.

Do you migrate email and Microsoft 365 mailboxes too?

Yes. We move email, contacts and calendars between accounts and into Microsoft 365, keeping your old folders and history intact. For larger or business mailbox moves, our Microsoft 365 support page covers the wider setup — we'll point you to the right plan rather than guess at a price here.

What does data migration cost?

Most migrations are quoted per job once we know what's involved, and for our monthly support customers this kind of help is often included in your plan. We don't publish a one-size price because moving a single laptop and migrating a business's email and files are very different jobs. Get in touch or book a service and we'll give you a clear quote or point you to the right plan.
