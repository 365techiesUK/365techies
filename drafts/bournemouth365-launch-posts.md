# Bournemouth365 web home — the launch sequence for the Facebook page (39K)

Written 19 Aug 2026. **Strategy: never announce a website.** The audience follows this page
for the seafront. So each post gives something genuinely useful *natively* (readable without
clicking), and the site is simply where it came from. One page introduced per post, over ~10
days, while the season still has hooks: **fireworks 21 + 28 Aug (the last two)**, **bank
holiday Mon 31 Aug**, schools back ~1 Sep.

Mechanics that matter:
- **Attach one of your own photos** (never a stock image, never a screenshot of the website).
  The photo does the reach; the link does the job.
- **Link in the post**, not hidden in the first comment. Reach is slightly lower; clicks and
  trust are higher, and we're not running a growth hack.
- **Pin post 1** to the top of the page for the fortnight.
- Post ~5–7pm for a local audience. Reply to every comment in the first hour — that's what
  makes Facebook show it to more people, more than any wording trick.
- Don't cross-post all four to the 365 Techies page. Post 1 and the signal-map one, only.

## What NOT to post
- No "we've redesigned our website" post. Ever.
- No estimated or modelled figures dressed as measured ones — this page's whole credibility is
  that the numbers come off instruments, timestamped.
- Nothing about the Air Festival returning (it isn't) and no tide-times page or webcam (we
  deliberately don't do those — don't imply we do).
- No IT sales pitch in posts 2–4. The 365 Techies mention in post 1 is enough for the fortnight.

## After the sequence
Keep the page fed with the **live** stuff — a sea temperature on a hot day, "fireworks tonight,
here's where to stand", a good sunrise — each with the relevant link. That's the habit that turns
39K followers into people who *use* the site, which is the only number that matters.
