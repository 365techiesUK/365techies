# Bournemouth365 web home — the launch sequence for the Facebook page (39K)

Written 19 Aug 2026. **REWRITTEN the same day: the fireworks are cancelled.** The 14 August
display was called off after the Government's national wildfire emergency alert, and the
organisers have since cancelled the rest of the season. The original post 1 was built entirely
around "two Fridays left" — it would have sent families to a dark beach. Binned.

**Strategy unchanged: never announce a website.** The audience follows this page for the
seafront. Each post gives something genuinely useful *natively* (readable without clicking),
and the site is simply where it came from.

Mechanics that matter:
- **Attach one of your own photos.** The photo does the reach; the link does the job.
- **Link in the post**, not hidden in the first comment.
- **Pin post 1** for the fortnight.
- Post ~5–7pm for a local audience. Reply to every comment in the first hour.

## What NOT to post
- No "we've redesigned our website" post. Ever.
- **Nothing that implies the fireworks are on.** No fireworks photo as a lead image until the
  2027 season is announced.
- No estimated or modelled figures dressed as measured ones — this page's credibility is that
  the numbers come off instruments, timestamped.
- Nothing about the Air Festival returning (it isn't), no tide-times page or webcam.
- No IT sales pitch in posts 2–4. The mention in post 1 is enough for the fortnight.

## After the sequence
Keep the page fed with the **live** stuff — a sea temperature on a hot day, a good sunrise, and
(when it returns) "fireworks tonight, here's where to stand". That habit is what turns 39K
followers into people who *use* the site.
