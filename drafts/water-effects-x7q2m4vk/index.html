Bridgewater water effects — try all three tiers

Three candidate treatments, cheapest last. Judge them on this iPad and on the desktop — especially how they feel after thirty seconds, not three. Everything here honours the system Reduce Motion setting: turn it on and the water goes still.

1 · The showpiece: interactive water ink homepage hero only · gated

Drag a finger (or the mouse) through the panel. This is a real fluid simulation running on the graphics chip — retuned from its neon defaults to slow brand-blue ink. In production it would sit behind the hero heading like this mock, load only after the page is fully interactive, pause when off-screen, and switch off entirely for Reduce-Motion users.

2 · The foundation: wave dividers every page · every device

Layered waves between sections, drifting very slowly (and standing still under Reduce Motion). Costs almost nothing, works on every phone, and carries the water theme site-wide even where the showpiece never runs. This is the tier the research recommends shipping first.
