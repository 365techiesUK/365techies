// INSPIRON DESKTOPS · WINDOWS 11

Dell Inspiron desktops and Windows 11 — which ones can upgrade?

Dell’s Inspiron small towers — the 3650, 3668, 3670, 3880 and their siblings — sat under thousands of Dorset desks, and owners are still asking the same question on Dell’s own forums: can mine run Windows 11? The honest answer splits cleanly in two. From the 3670 onwards, yes — they’re on Dell’s tested list. The 3650 and 3668 have no supported route at all, and no processor swap changes that. Here’s how to check yours in 30 seconds, and the sensible options whichever side of the line you land on. Rated 4.9 on Google.

// THE VERDICT

The straight answer, model by model

Dell sold the Inspiron small tower for over a decade, and the model numbers all look alike — but Windows 11 draws a hard line through the middle of the family. Microsoft’s processor floor starts at Intel’s 8th generation, and Dell’s own tested-models list (Dell knowledge base article 000187485) confirms which desktops made the cut.

The check below tells you about your machine, and it takes half a minute.

// CHECK YOURS

The 30-second check that settles it

You don’t need to open the case or install anything. Three built-in tools give you the answer:

  • Read the processor line. Press Win+R, type msinfo32, press Enter, and look at the Processor entry (Settings > System > About shows the same thing). For an Intel Core chip, the digits after the dash are what matter: 8xxx or higher passes Microsoft’s floor (an i3-8100 or i5-8400, say); 7xxx or lower fails (an i5-7400 or i5-6400), and no setting anywhere changes that.
  • Check the security chip. Press Win+R, type tpm.msc. “Specification Version: 2.0” means you’re ready. If it says a compatible TPM cannot be found, don’t panic — on a Dell that usually means Intel PTT is simply switched off. Restart, tap F2 into the BIOS, and enable it under Security > PTT Security (Dell’s article 000189676 covers this).
  • Get the final verdict. Microsoft’s free PC Health Check app gives a plain yes or no, and you can cross-check your Service Tag at dell.com/support against Dell’s tested list.

If msinfo32 feels daunting, our free PC spec checker shows your processor model right in the browser — one of the free tools on this site. And if you’d rather just ask, call 01202 775566 and read us the line.

// THE HARD TRUTH

Why there’s no upgrade path for the 3650 and 3668

With plenty of older desktops there’s a clever hardware answer — our OptiPlex 9020 guide walks through exactly that kind of thinking. We’d love to tell you the same story here, but honesty comes first: for the Inspiron 3650 and 3668 there is no supported hardware path to Windows 11 at all.

The reason is the motherboard, not just the chip that happens to be in it. The 3668 is built on Intel’s H110 chipset, which only accepts 6th and 7th-generation processors — owners on Dell’s own community forum confirm it “only takes 6th or 7th gen”, and the hardware databases agree. Windows 11’s floor starts at the 8th generation, so even the best processor the board can physically take still fails the check. The 3650 is a generation older again, from the DDR3L era, and the same logic applies. Neither model appears on Dell’s tested list, and Dell isn’t updating drivers for them.

Could you swap the motherboard, processor and memory together? Technically that’s building a different computer inside an old case — and by the time it’s done, it costs more than a good refurbished business desktop that already runs Windows 11. We won’t sell you that job, and we’d be wary of anyone who would. As independent Dell specialists we make nothing from telling you this — which is rather the point.

// ELIGIBLE MODELS

3670 onwards: upgrade normally — in the sensible order

If your tower is a 3670, 3671, 3880, 3881, 3891, 3910 or 3020 — or the check above passed — the upgrade itself is straightforward. The sensible order:

  • Back up first. Upgrades almost always go smoothly, but “almost always” isn’t a backup strategy. Copy your documents and photos somewhere safe before you start.
  • Confirm TPM and Secure Boot. Run tpm.msc as above; if PTT needed enabling in the BIOS, do that first, then re-run PC Health Check.
  • Take the upgrade. Windows Update offers Windows 11 to eligible machines; Microsoft’s Installation Assistant does the same job if the offer hasn’t appeared. Your files and programs stay in place.

// IF YOURS FAILS

Three honest options for a 3650 or 3668

First, the reassurance: an ineligible desktop is not instantly unsafe. Windows 10 reached end of support on 14 October 2025, but Microsoft’s consumer Extended Security Updates picked up where it left off — and that programme ends on 12 October 2027, so it buys time rather than a future. That gives you time to decide calmly. Your three real options:

  • Keep it on Windows 10 with ESU. Enrol, keep receiving security updates, and revisit the decision nearer 2027. Our ESU-or-upgrade guide walks through the whole decision, including how to enrol.
  • Keep it useful for lighter duties. A machine that can’t run Windows 11 can still be a perfectly good Linux desktop, an offline workhorse for an older program, or a spare — especially with the cheap speed boost described below.
  • Replace it like for like. A refurbished business-grade Dell OptiPlex from £545 — with a brand-new 1TB Samsung 990 PRO SSD, Windows 11, full setup and your data moved across, all included — is the natural successor to an Inspiron tower, and usually a sturdier machine than the one it replaces.

One thing we don’t recommend: the registry workarounds that force Windows 11 onto unsupported hardware. They exist, but Microsoft doesn’t guarantee updates on unsupported installs — the wrong trade for a computer you rely on.

// STILL GOOD KIT

A 3668 is still a perfectly good computer

“No Windows 11” doesn’t mean “worthless”. If your 3650 or 3668 is staying in service on Windows 10 ESU or as a Linux machine, it’s worth knowing what the hardware can honestly do — the figures below come from the published hardware databases for these models:

  • The single biggest win is an SSD. The 3668 typically shipped with a 1TB 3.5-inch hard drive and has no M.2 slot — but it has a spare SATA port, so a 2.5-inch SATA SSD drops in alongside and transforms how the machine feels. The 3650 takes the same treatment.
  • Memory: the 3668 has two DIMM slots of DDR4-2400 with an official 16GB maximum; the 3650 is similar but uses older DDR3L, also 16GB max. If yours has 4GB, a modest top-up helps.
  • Graphics, with a caveat: the 3668’s 240W power supply can support cards up to around a GTX 1650 Super, though some cards need a PCIe power adapter. Honestly, though — we’d think twice before spending serious money on a machine this age.

That’s the honest test for any spend on these towers: a cheap SSD to see out its ESU years, yes; a big investment, no. We fit SSDs and memory with free local collection — diagnosis first, then a quote, so you can judge the numbers before committing a penny.

// GOOD QUESTIONS

Frequently asked

Can the Dell Inspiron 3650 be upgraded to Windows 11?

No — and unusually, there’s no supported hardware route either. The 3650 uses Intel 6th-generation processors from the DDR3L era, below Microsoft’s 8th-generation floor, and its motherboard can’t take a chip that qualifies. It isn’t on Dell’s tested list. The good news: Microsoft’s free consumer Extended Security Updates keep Windows 10 patched to 12 October 2027, so you have time to plan rather than panic.

Can the Dell Inspiron 3668 run Windows 11?

No. The 3668 is built on Intel’s H110 chipset, which only accepts 6th and 7th-generation processors — and Windows 11 requires 8th generation or newer. So even the best processor the board can physically take still fails the check, and Dell hasn’t tested or listed the model for Windows 11. Your honest options are Windows 10 ESU (free consumer coverage to 12 October 2027), keeping it for lighter duties, or replacing it.

Can’t I just swap in a newer processor?

Not on the 3650 or 3668 — the motherboard chipset sets a hard ceiling at Intel’s 6th/7th generation, and Windows 11’s floor starts at the 8th. Swapping the board, processor and memory together is technically possible, but that’s building a new computer in an old case, and it costs more than a refurbished business desktop that already runs Windows 11. We won’t sell you that job.

My Inspiron desktop says “This PC can’t run Windows 11” — what does that actually mean?

It means the machine fails at least one of Microsoft’s requirements — usually the processor generation, sometimes just a TPM setting that’s switched off. On 3670-and-newer towers it’s often fixable in the BIOS by enabling Intel PTT; on a 3650 or 3668 the processor itself is the blocker and no setting changes it. Our guide to that exact error message walks through telling the two cases apart.

Which Inspiron desktops are on Dell’s Windows 11 tested list?

From the small-tower family: the 3670, 3671, 3880, 3881, 3891, 3910 and 3020, plus the slimline 3470 and 3471 — all listed in Dell’s tested-models article (knowledge base 000187485). The 3647, 3650, 3656 and 3668 are absent. Even for listed models it’s worth running the 30-second check on your own unit, because configurations varied over each production run.

Is my Windows 10 Inspiron desktop unsafe now?

Not instantly, no. Windows 10 reached end of support on 14 October 2025, but Microsoft’s consumer Extended Security Updates carried on from there — originally to 12 October 2027, and Microsoft has since extended free consumer coverage to 12 October 2027. An enrolled machine keeps receiving security patches. What we’d gently discourage is drifting past that date without a plan — our ESU-or-upgrade guide covers the decision properly.

What about the registry trick to install Windows 11 anyway?

It exists, and it works in the narrow sense that Windows 11 will install. But Microsoft doesn’t guarantee updates on unsupported hardware, so you could be trading a patched, supported Windows 10 for an unpatched Windows 11 — a poor swap for a computer you rely on. For a hobby or experiment machine, that’s your call; for the family desktop or the office PC, we don’t recommend it.
