# ⚠️ THE Crawl-delay LINE BELOW IS LOAD-BEARING. DO NOT DELETE IT.
# SiteGround answers any request URI containing "robots.txt" from a platform
# handler (/myrobots.txt and /xrobots.txt get answered too, and neither exists)
# and injects its server default of 10 if we don't state a value. Deleting our
# line does not remove the throttle — it restores 10. Their KB documents the
# 10 default and a minimum permitted value of 1, and a probe on 2026-08-02
# confirmed a supplied 1 is served back unchanged. 1 is the floor; take it.
#
# How it was found: /robots.txt returns no ETag and no Last-Modified while
# /llms.txt, /sitemap.xml and / all return both — a server cannot fingerprint a
# body it is generating. Verify from a REAL BROWSER: scripted requests get the
# WAF (403, or 202 + sgcaptcha) and you will measure the challenge page instead.
#
# Impact, stated honestly after three earlier claims here turned out wrong:
# Google ignores crawl-delay outright, Yandex dropped it in 2018, and every AI
# crawler below has its OWN group — under RFC 9309 a crawler obeys only its most
# specific matching group, so none of them ever reads this wildcard group. The
# cost is Bing freshness and the surfaces behind Bing. At 670 URLs this takes a
# full pass from ~1h52m down to ~11 minutes.
