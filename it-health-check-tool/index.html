// HOW IT WORKS

How to run a free IT health check for your business

A free small-business IT health check is a quick set of plain-English questions on your backups, passwords, MFA, updates and security that gives an instant on-screen score out of 100 plus a short list of the gaps worth fixing first.

  • Score bands: 80-100 Strong · 55-79 Good, with some gaps · under 55 Needs attention.
  • Areas checked (all users): backups, passwords, multi-factor login (MFA), updates, Windows version, anti-malware, spotting scams/phishing, email & Microsoft 365, Wi-Fi/router security, and who fixes problems.
  • Business adds: staff leaver process, working towards Cyber Essentials, and GDPR/compliance confidence.
  • Home adds: whether the whole family's devices are set up safely.
  • Runs entirely in your browser - no sign-up, nothing stored - and lists your top gaps to fix first.

Step by step

  1. Choose home or business — Open the tool and select business (or home) so the questions adapt to you - business adds checks on staff leavers, security standards and GDPR.
  2. Answer honestly — Work through the short multiple-choice questions on backups, passwords, MFA, updates, Windows version, anti-malware, phishing awareness, email, Wi-Fi and who fixes things.
  3. Get your score out of 100 — The tool tallies your answers instantly in your browser and shows a score with a band: Strong, Good with some gaps, or Needs attention.
  4. Read your top priorities — See a ranked shortlist of the biggest gaps, each linking to the fix, so you know what to tackle first.
  5. Book a real review if needed — For a proper look, book a free IT health check and a 365 Techies techie reviews your security, backups and setup properly on a free call.

Good to know

Is this free IT health check suitable for a UK small business?

Yes. Choose 'business' at the start and the questions adapt to include staff leaver processes, working towards Cyber Essentials and GDPR data handling - the areas that matter most for a UK small business, alongside backups, MFA and updates.

Does the IT health check send my answers to 365 Techies?

No. The check runs entirely in your browser and scores instantly on screen - nothing is stored or sent unless you choose to contact us for a full report. There's no sign-up and no email required to see your score.

What counts as a good IT health score?

The tool scores you out of 100: 80 or above is 'Strong', 55 to 79 is 'Good, with some gaps', and below 55 is 'Needs attention'. Whatever your score, it lists the specific gaps worth fixing first.
