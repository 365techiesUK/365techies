/02 — FINDABILITY FIRST

A beautiful website nobody finds is a brochure in a drawer

In a study of four million Google searches, barely 1 in 200 people clicked anything on page two. Studies consistently show the top three results take between half and two-thirds of all clicks. If your site isn’t findable, it may as well not exist — however lovely it looks.

Google confirms that page experience signals like Core Web Vitals are used by its ranking systems. To be honest with you (as always): speed alone won’t put you at #1 — Google says relevance comes first. But where competing local sites are otherwise similar, great page experience contributes — and that’s the edge we build in from day one.

And the field is genuinely weak: Google’s own real-user data (the Core Web Vitals Technology Report) shows fewer than half of WordPress sites — the platform much of the web is built on — pass Core Web Vitals, and a large share of Wix and Squarespace sites fail too. Those are real-visitor measurements, not lab scores. Don’t take our word for it: test any site against ours, above.

  • Technically SEO-sound from day one
  • Fast pages that pass Core Web Vitals
  • Clean structure Google can read
  • Mobile-first, accessible design
  • Honest about what SEO can’t promise
  • Findability baked in, not bolted on

/03 — HONEST VALUE

What do websites really cost?

Published UK rates, not sales talk — so you can judge any quote (including ours) with the numbers in front of you.

  • A typical small-business site

    Roughly £2,500–£10,000 before hosting and upkeep, according to independent UK guides from Duport, Forbes Advisor UK and GoDaddy’s own 2026 guide.

  • A bespoke 10–20 page site

    Regional agencies’ own published packages run about £3,000–£15,000; London full-service firms quote £8,000–£30,000+ for a similar brief.

  • The words on the pages

    Professional copywriting starts around £100 per 1,000-word page, and the average UK freelance copywriter’s day rate is £480 (ProCopywriters survey, 2025). Fifty proper pages is a five-figure line on its own.

  • Calculators, checkers & tools

    Custom interactive tools are software, not web design — UK bespoke-software guides price customer-facing tools from £30,000+.

  • The bills that follow

    Agencies typically charge 15–25% of the build cost per year in maintenance, plus SEO retainers of £700–£1,500+ a month.

  • The scope test: this site

    What you’re reading is a 475+ page site with 20+ free tools and live energy dashboards. At the published rates above, that’s a six-figure project — we built it in-house, and you can test the result live on this page.

Paying agency prices isn’t wrong — paying them without proof is. Plenty of expensive websites score poorly on the very tests Google publishes; you can check any of them — and the designer’s own website — on this page in thirty seconds.

// GOOD QUESTIONS

Frequently asked

Do you design and build websites?

Yes — we design and build fast, modern, mobile-friendly websites for sole traders and small businesses across Bournemouth, Poole and Dorset, then host, secure and look after them for you. Got an existing site that isn’t working? See our website rebuild service.

Do you do SEO as well as design?

Findability is the whole point — there’s no point having a beautiful website if nobody can find it. Every site we build is technically SEO-sound from day one (that’s the SEO 100 on our own Lighthouse report — test it on this page), with clean structure, proper metadata and genuinely fast pages.

Can I see proof your sites are fast?

Yes — right on this page. Run our own website through the free checker above and the scores are measured live by Google’s Lighthouse engine while you watch. We’d rather show you than make claims.

How fast will my site be?

We build to the same standard as our own site — but we won’t promise you a number, because honest people can’t: scores vary between runs and depend on your content. What we can say: Google confirms page experience signals like Core Web Vitals are used by its ranking systems, and relevance always comes first — so we build for both.

How much does a website cost?

Independent UK guides put a typical agency-built small-business website at roughly £2,500–£10,000 before hosting. Every project is different, so we quote fixed and up front — tell us what you need and you’ll get a clear written price with no surprises, from a local firm you can actually visit.

Why do some websites cost £50,000 or even more?

Because past a certain size a website is really software plus a publishing operation. At published UK rates, professionally written pages start around £100 each, custom checkers and calculators are £30,000+ software projects, and maintenance adds 15–25% a year on top. Our own 475-page site with its 20+ free tools is exactly that scope — built in-house, which is why we can build yours to the same measurable standard for a sensible fixed price.

Where are the websites hosted?

On premium infrastructure powered by SiteGround — ultrafast cloud hosting with free SSL, daily backups, a global CDN and a UK data centre, all managed by us.

Can you set up professional business email?

Yes — we set up email on your own domain (you@yourbusiness.co.uk) or Microsoft 365, with spam protection and configuration on all your devices.

Do you look after the website after launch?

Absolutely — our premium plan includes managed hosting, updates, security, backups and support, so your site stays fast, safe and online.
