/06 — POCKET & PLANET

Good for your wallet, good for the planet

Most of a computer’s carbon footprint comes from making it, not using it. Choosing refurbished gives a perfectly good Dell a second life instead of sending it to landfill — and the UK is the second-worst country in the world for e-waste per person, so it genuinely matters.

An independent Cranfield University study (2023, with Circular Computing) found a professionally remanufactured laptop produced only about 6% of the carbon of building a brand-new one. Choosing refurbished is one of the easiest green wins there is — more on our sustainability page.

/07 — BUSINESS vs CONSUMER

Why a business-grade Dell beats a cheap new machine

Latitude laptops and OptiPlex PCs were built for offices that can’t afford downtime — not built down to a price for a shop shelf. Here’s the difference you actually feel.

Business-grade Dell (what we supply)

  • Tougher build — metal frames, spill-resistant keyboards; Latitudes tested to military-standard durability methods
  • Built to run all day, for years — not months
  • Stronger security built in (TPM chip, encryption)
  • Designed to be opened, serviced and upgraded
  • Quality components chosen for reliability, not the lowest price
  • Plus our new Samsung Pro SSD, warranties and local support

A typical new consumer laptop or PC

  • Often plastic, built down to a price point
  • Tends to slow down and wear out sooner
  • Basic security, more bloatware to slow it down
  • Frequently sealed shut — hard to repair or upgrade
  • Cheapest components that meet the price
  • A box on a shelf — no one local behind it

The upshot: a refurbished business Dell often outlives — and outperforms — a brand-new budget machine that costs about the same.

// GOOD QUESTIONS

Frequently asked

Are these new or refurbished?

They’re professionally refurbished — genuine ex-business Dell Latitude laptops and OptiPlex desktops that we test, securely wipe, clean, grade and set up before they go out. Not new, and we’d never pretend otherwise — that’s rather the point: proper business-grade kit for a lot less.

How much do refurbished Dells cost?

Our refurbished Dell Latitude laptops and OptiPlex desktops start from £299, with the exact price depending on the model, spec and condition grade you choose. Tell us your budget and what you’ll use it for, and we’ll match you the best machine for the money — every one tested, set up, supported and backed by our warranties.

I’m not very confident with technology — is this still for me?

Absolutely — you don’t need to know any of the technical words, that’s our job, not yours. We help plenty of people who’d rather not deal with the technical side. We choose the right computer for you, set it all up, and can make it easier to use with larger text and other accessibility options. And there’s always a real local person to phone if you ever get stuck — the same friendly faces, year after year, on a number that’s been ours for years (01202 775566).

Are refurbished business laptops any good?

Genuinely, yes. Business-grade Dell Latitudes and OptiPlexes are built to work hard for years — tougher materials and, for Latitude laptops, tested to military-standard durability methods. A well-refurbished one often outlasts a brand-new budget laptop that was built down to a price.

Is it safe to buy refurbished?

From us, yes. Every machine is fully tested and securely wiped, you’re buying from a real local firm — a Dorset family business you can phone, text or email, here since 1995 — and you’re covered by our own warranty plus your Consumer Rights Act protection, very different from an untested bargain off a marketplace.

What do the condition grades mean?

We grade Dell Latitude laptops A, B or C by how they look — A is near-mint, B has light cosmetic wear, C has more visible marks — not by how they work. Every grade is fully tested and working, and we tell you the exact grade before you buy.

What about the storage and battery?

Every machine is fitted with a brand-new Samsung Pro SSD (with a 5-year guarantee) for speed and reliability. Laptop batteries wear with use, so we check each one and tell you its condition up front — and can fit a fresh battery if you’d like.

What warranty do I get?

Plenty of cover: a Dell next-business-day worldwide warranty (extendable if you need it), our own 365 Techies 5-year guarantee, and the new Samsung Pro SSD’s 5-year guarantee — plus full support on any of our plans and a full repair service if you ever break it. As a UK buyer you’re also covered by the Consumer Rights Act, including a 30-day right to reject if something’s faulty.

Can you supply screens, webcams and other accessories?

Yes — we’ll supply and set up whatever you need alongside your Dell: monitors, webcams, backup drives, speakers, keyboards, mice and more, all configured and ready to use.

Can you wipe and recycle my old computer?

Yes — we can move your files across to your new Dell, then securely wipe (with a certificate) and responsibly recycle your old machine. See our secure IT disposal page.

Do you set it up and support it too?

Always. Every Dell we supply is set up ready to use — Windows, Microsoft 365, security, backups and your data moved across — and you can keep it looked after on a home or business support plan.

Do you supply second-hand computers for a business or office?

Yes — refurbished business-grade Dell OptiPlex desktops and Latitude laptops are ideal, affordable office machines. See our page on second-hand office computers in Poole, or setting up a new laptop ready to use for home.
