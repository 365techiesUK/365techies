WHY IT WORKS

Why an SSD is the single biggest speed upgrade

If your laptop or desktop has slowed to a crawl — long boot times, the spinning circle, programs that take an age to open — the cause is almost always the old mechanical hard drive (HDD) inside it. A traditional hard drive has physical spinning platters and a moving read head, and that mechanism is the single slowest part of the whole computer.

A solid-state drive (SSD) has no moving parts at all. Swapping the old drive for an SSD is, by a wide margin, the biggest real-world speed improvement you can make — usually far more noticeable than adding memory or a faster processor. On the same machine you can expect to see:

  • Windows starting in seconds rather than minutes
  • Programs, browsers and files opening almost instantly
  • An end to the “busy” freezes when several things run at once
  • A quieter, cooler, longer-lasting machine with no spinning disk

A five- or six-year-old computer with an SSD often feels quicker for everyday work than a brand-new one still using a mechanical drive. It really is the closest thing to a new-computer feeling without buying a new computer.

If your machine is sluggish in general, our slow computer repair service looks at every possible cause — but the drive is by far the most common culprit, and this page is all about that one big fix.

THE UPGRADE

A brand-new Samsung Pro SSD, everything cloned across

We don’t fit bargain-bin drives. Every upgrade uses a brand-new Samsung Pro SSD, one of the most reliable drives made, backed by a 5-year manufacturer guarantee. It’s the same quality of drive we put in the refurbished machines we sell.

The part people worry about most is losing everything — and that’s exactly what we prevent. We clone your whole system across to the new SSD, so:

  • Windows stays exactly as it was — no reinstall, no re-activation
  • All your programs, files, photos, emails and settings come with you
  • You switch on afterwards and everything is where you left it — just far faster

STRAIGHT TALK

Upgrade or replace? Our honest steer

We won’t sell you an upgrade that isn’t worth it. Before we fit anything we check the whole machine — not just the drive — and give you an honest steer:

  • An SSD is well worth it when the computer is otherwise sound: a decent processor, enough memory, a good screen and body, and it’s the slow drive holding it back. This covers the vast majority of Dell Latitude and OptiPlex machines from the last several years.
  • Replacement makes more sense when the machine is very old or very underpowered, the battery is shot and the hinges are going, or the repair bill would approach the cost of a better machine. In that case we’ll point you at a refurbished Dell laptop or refurbished desktop from £545 — which already comes with a new Samsung Pro SSD fitted.
