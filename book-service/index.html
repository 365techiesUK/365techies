// GOOD QUESTIONS

Frequently asked

Can I reschedule or cancel my appointment?

Yes — booking sets up your own customer portal, and you can move or cancel an appointment there in a couple of taps. Or just call 01202 775566 and we'll do it for you.

What can I book online?

Computer and laptop servicing, repairs, new device setup, security checks and on-site visits across Bournemouth, Poole and Dorset. Not sure what you need? Call us on 01202 775566.

Do I need an account to book?

There's no password to invent. You pick a time, then we email you a 6-digit code to check we've got your address right — that also creates your customer portal, where you can manage the appointment afterwards. Next time you book, you're already signed in.

Will I know when you're coming?

Yes — for on-site visits we phone you when we're on our way and give you an estimated arrival time, so you know exactly when to expect us. For remote sessions we call before we connect to check you're ready, and we never connect out of the blue.

Not sure what you need?

Call us on 01202 775566 or start a live chat and a friendly techie will help you book the right appointment.
