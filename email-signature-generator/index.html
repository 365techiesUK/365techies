// GOOD QUESTIONS

Frequently asked

How do I add my signature to Outlook?

In new Outlook / Outlook on the web: Settings → Account → Signatures → paste into the box and save. In classic Outlook: File → Options → Mail → Signatures → New → paste. Click “Copy signature” above first, then paste with Ctrl+V.

How do I add it to Gmail?

Gmail → Settings (gear) → See all settings → General → scroll to Signature → Create new → paste it in, then scroll down and Save Changes.

How do I add it to Apple Mail?

Mail → Settings → Signatures → choose your account → + → paste. Untick “Always match my default message font” so the formatting is kept.

Why does my signature look different in some emails?

Email apps are fussy — that’s why this generator builds signatures the old-school reliable way (tables, inline styles, standard fonts) rather than fancy code that breaks. Keep images out of signatures unless they’re hosted properly, and it’ll look right everywhere.

What should a UK business include in its signature?

Many UK limited companies include the registered company name, company number and registered office in email footers — the “extra line” field is ideal for this. If in doubt, check with your accountant.

Can you set up signatures for my whole team?

Yes — we roll out consistent, centrally-managed signatures across Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace so every mailbox matches and nobody can break them. Ask us.

// HOW IT WORKS

How to make a UK business email signature with your company number

This free generator builds an email-safe HTML signature for a UK business, and you add your registered company name, company number and registered office in the 'Extra line' field so they appear in your footer.

Step by step

  1. Fill in your details — Enter your name, job title, company, phone, email and website in the form — the live preview updates as you type.
  2. Add your company number in the Extra line — In the 'Extra line' field, type your legal footer, for example 'Registered in England & Wales No. 1234567, [registered office]'.
  3. Choose a style and colour — Pick the Classic accent-bar or Compact one-line style and set your brand accent colour.
  4. Copy the signature — Click the copy button to grab the finished, email-safe signature (built with reliable tables and inline styles).
  5. Paste it into your email app — Paste it into your signature settings in Outlook, Gmail or your email client and save.

Good to know

Is there a dedicated company-number field?

Not a separate box — you add your company number using the free-text 'Extra line' field, which is designed exactly for this. Its placeholder even shows the format: 'Registered in England & Wales No. 1234567'.

Where does the Extra line appear in the signature?

It renders as a small grey line at the very bottom of the signature, below your contact details, in both the Classic (accent-bar) and Compact (one-line) styles — the right place for a legal footer that shouldn't dominate.

Can I add my VAT number too?

Yes — the Extra line is free text, so you can include a VAT number alongside the company number if you're VAT-registered. Keep it to one clear line so it renders reliably across email apps.
