Home / Email Support / We Move Your Virgin Email

Move your Virgin email to Gmail, no computer needed

Do these steps on your iPhone, iPad, Android phone or computer, then text us two codes. We move every email into Gmail for you, and give you a list of every place that has your old address.

★ 4.9 on GoogleFamily-run since 1995Nothing on Virgin is deleted

An independent service from the 365 Techies team. We are not Virgin Media or Junara, and we never ask for your Virgin password, only the two codes on this page. · Reviewed 1 November 2026

How it works

Three parts, and you only do two of them

  1. 1. Get readySteps 1 to 3: the app, a Gmail address and Google’s security check.Once, any time · about 15 minutes
  2. 2. Make two codes and text them to usSteps 4 to 6, all in one go.On a weekday · about 5 minutes
  3. 3. We do the moveWe copy every folder into Gmail and send you your change list: every place to give your new address. Then steps 7 to 11.A few days, without you

The move costs £60 per email address, agreed before we start. We send the invoice when the move is finished, and you pay by bank transfer.

No Windows PC, just a phone, tablet or Mac? This is the way: our app is for Windows PCs, so we do the move for you, on our own computers. Got a Windows PC and want to do it yourself? Our free 365 PC Manager app moves it for you, free.

Before you start, check this is for you

  • Your address ends in virginmedia.com, blueyonder.co.uk, ntlworld.com or virgin.net.
  • It’s the main email on your Virgin Media account. Virgin won’t make the code we need for an extra mailbox on the account, so if yours is an extra one, text us first.
  • You can sign in to the My Virgin Media app. Virgin only makes the code in that app.
  • Your mailbox hasn’t moved to Junara yet. If it has, text us first: it still works, but the steps are different.
  • You have a mobile phone for Google’s security texts.

Part 1 · any time

Get ready

  1. 1

    Get the My Virgin Media app and sign in iPhone, iPad, Android

    On an iPhone, find My Virgin Media in the App Store. On an Android phone, find it in Google Play. Sign in with your My Virgin Media username and password.

    Then check the code is on offer. Tap Account settings, then Account details, and look under Virgin Media Mail for Manage your Virgin Media Mail app password. Don’t tap Generate yet. If it isn’t there, stop and text us: Virgin only offers it for the main email on the account.

    Only have an iPad? Look for it in the App Store on your iPad. If it isn’t there, or it won’t sign you in, text us before going any further.

  2. 2

    Have a Gmail address

    Already use Gmail? Skip this step.

    If not, make one in the Gmail app or at accounts.google.com. Choose an address you’re happy to keep for good, and keep its password safe.

  3. 3

    Turn on Google’s 2-Step Verification

    Google only gives out the code we need once this is on. Open myaccount.google.com, search for 2-Step Verification and follow the steps. Google texts a code to your mobile to check it’s you.

    Then check Google will make the code. Open myaccount.google.com/apppasswords. If you see a box to type an app name, you’re ready: don’t create one yet. If it says the setting isn’t available for your account, text us.

    Already on? Skip this step. Google’s own help: turn on 2-Step Verification. This is the step most people get stuck on: text us if you do.

Part 2 · on a weekday, all in one go

Make two codes and text them to us

Do steps 4, 5 and 6 together, Monday to Friday, 9 to 5, so we can start straight away. Virgin gives each mailbox only one code, shared by all your email apps, so making a new one (step 5) cancels the old one. If the Mail app on your iPhone or iPad then asks for your Virgin password, type the new code in there too: the same code works for your Mail app and for us at the same time.

  1. 4

    Make your Google code

    Open myaccount.google.com/apppasswords and sign in if it asks. Type a name such as 365 Mail Mover and tap Create.

    Google shows a code of 16 letters. Copy it (press and hold it, then tap Copy) or write it down before you close the box.

  2. 5

    Make your Virgin code

    In the My Virgin Media app, tap Account settings, then Account details. Under Virgin Media Mail, tap Manage, then Generate new App password.

    Copy the password it shows, or write it down.

    If your Mail app asks for your Virgin password afterwards, type this same code in there.

  3. 6

    Text us both codes

    Send one text to 07520 615332 with these four things:

    Your Virgin email addressYour Gmail addressThe Google code (16 letters)The Virgin code

    Text us: 07520 615332Or ring 01202 775566 and read them out

    Only ever send these two codes, never your normal passwords. They only work in email programs, and you cancel both when we’re done.

Part 3 · we take it from here

What we do

  • We check both codes straight away, and text you if one doesn’t work.
  • We copy every folder off Virgin, usually within the hour. Nothing on Virgin is deleted, moved or marked as read. Spam and Bin are left out.
  • Gmail takes them in over the next few days, because Google limits how much it accepts each day. They appear under a label called Virgin Media, with their folders, dates, and read and unread kept.
  • We send you your change list: every organisation that emails your old address, the important ones first (more below).
  • We text you when it’s finished, after checking every folder arrived.

Included with your move

Your change list: every place that has your old address

If you’ve had your Virgin address for years, your bank, your doctor, the council and your energy supplier all write to it. Changing it can feel like changing your phone number, so we make it easy to see what needs doing.

While we copy your email, we note every organisation that has written to your old address. When the move is done we send you the list, the important ones first, with a box to tick as you give each one your new address. It comes as a PDF you can print or keep on your tablet.

Do these first

  • Your bank214 emails · last Oct 2026
  • Your building society96 emails · last Oct 2026
  • Home insurance38 emails · last Sep 2026
  • Your GP surgery18 emails · last Oct 2026
  • The council15 emails · last Sep 2026
  • Your energy supplier72 emails · last Oct 2026

… then shopping, apps and subscriptions, travel and everyone else.

A made-up example

What’s on it

  • Every organisation that emails you, grouped: banks and money, insurance and pensions, health, government and council, and bills first.
  • How many emails each has sent, and when the last one came, so you can see which matter.
  • The ones you haven’t heard from in years, set apart: probably no longer needed.
  • A reminder of the ones that often write by post, like your pension, the DVLA and your GP, so nothing is missed.
  • How to change it safely: always on their own website or app, never through a link in an email or text.

Friends and family aren’t listed: we just tell you how many people write to you, and the easy way to give them all your new address at once.

Part 3 · when we text you

Finish off

  1. 7

    Cancel the Virgin code when we say it’s copied

    Do this as soon as we text that your email is copied. In the My Virgin Media app, go back to the same place as step 5 and tap Generate new App password again. Making it cancels the code you sent us.

    Still reading your Virgin email in your phone or tablet’s Mail app? Type this newest code in there when it asks.

  2. 8

    Read your email in Gmail

    Install the Gmail app from the App Store or Google Play and sign in. Your old emails appear under Virgin Media as Gmail takes them in.

  3. 9

    Send new Virgin email on to Gmail

    In Safari or Chrome, go to mail.virginmedia.com and sign in. Tap the Settings cog, then Email, then Auto Forward. Switch it on, type your Gmail address, switch on Keep a copy, and tap Apply changes.

    Stuck? Text us and we’ll talk you through it.

  4. 10

    Cancel the Google code when it’s finished

    When we text that the move is finished, open myaccount.google.com/apppasswords and tap the bin next to 365 Mail Mover.

  5. 11

    Work through your change list

    Give each place on your change list your new address, the important ones first, and tick them off. There’s no rush to do them all in one day.

    More help: change your email address everywhere.

// GOOD QUESTIONS

Frequently asked

Do I need a computer?

No. You do the steps on this page on your iPhone, iPad or Android phone. We do the move itself on our own computers, and nothing is ever installed on your phone or tablet.

Is it safe to text you the two codes?

They are app passwords: they only work in email programs, they can’t be used to sign in to your Virgin or Google account or change anything, and you cancel both when we’re done (steps 7 and 10). Never send us your normal passwords. Once we’ve told you both codes work, you can delete the text.

How will I know everywhere I’ve used my old email address?

That’s what your change list is for. While we copy your email, we note every organisation that has written to your old address. When the move is done we send you the list as a PDF, with banks, insurance, health, the council and bills first, and a box to tick for each. It also reminds you of the ones that often write by post, like your pension and the DVLA.

Will I lose any email?

No. Nothing in your Virgin mailbox is deleted, moved or marked as read: we only copy it. Every folder arrives in Gmail under a label called Virgin Media, with its dates and read and unread kept. Spam and Bin are left out.

Why does my iPhone or iPad ask for my Virgin password?

Virgin gives each mailbox just one app password, shared by every email app you use, and making a new one (step 5) cancels the old one. So a Mail app that was using the old one asks again. Type the new code in and it carries on as before: the same code works in your Mail app and for us at the same time. You can also read your email at mail.virginmedia.com.

What if I can’t find the app password in the My Virgin Media app?

Virgin only offers it for the main email address on the account, not for extra mailboxes. That’s why step 1 checks for it first. If it isn’t there, stop and text us. With a Windows PC we can still move it another way. With only a phone or tablet, we’ll tell you straight if there’s no way to do it.

What if I get stuck?

Stop at that step and text or ring us: nothing is lost by stopping. Most people get stuck on Google’s 2-Step Verification, and we can talk you through it.

My mailbox has already moved to Junara

Then you don’t need the Virgin code. Text us before you start: the move still works, but the steps are different. What the Junara change means.

Stuck on a step?

Stop where you are and text or ring us. Real people answer, Monday to Friday, 9 to 5.
