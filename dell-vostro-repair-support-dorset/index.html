Who we are

Independent Dell Vostro specialists for Dorset small businesses

Vostro was Dell’s small-business range for nearly two decades, and Dorset is full of them — Vostro 3000-series laptops on reception desks, 5000-series machines in accounts, Vostro towers quietly running the office. When one goes wrong, it’s rarely just an inconvenience: it’s an invoice that can’t be sent or a till that can’t take payments.

We’re 365 Techies — a family-run Bournemouth IT firm going since 1995. Two things you should know up front. First, we are independent. We are not authorised, certified or endorsed by Dell. Our only formal Dell relationship is that we’ve been an independent Dell reseller since 2001, which lets us order new Dell machines at trade when a replacement genuinely makes sense. Second, we have no walk-in shop — we come to you instead. That means free collection of faulty machines across Bournemouth, Poole, Christchurch and the wider Dorset area, on-site visits for businesses, remote support for anything fixable over a secure connection, and by-appointment meetings at Kinson Community Centre if you’d rather sit down face to face.

We won’t promise what we can’t deliver: no 24/7 helpline, no guaranteed same-day fixes. What you get is honest diagnosis, real prices, and the same faces every time — the things our 4.9-star Google reviews keep mentioning. For the wider picture of what we do with Dell machines, see our Dell IT support hub.

The honest-broker bit

Dell discontinued Vostro. We didn’t.

In January 2025 Dell retired the Vostro name, folding its small-business role into the new “Dell Pro” family. Existing warranties are being honoured, but no new Vostros are being sold — which leaves thousands of UK small businesses running machines that are officially yesterday’s line, wondering whether that means they should bin perfectly good computers.

Our answer: almost never, purely on the badge. A discontinued name doesn’t make the hardware worse overnight. Most Vostro faults we see — tired batteries, worn charging ports, clogged coolers, slow old hard drives — are routine, economical repairs on a machine that then carries on for years.

Here’s why you can trust our advice on this: we don’t stock Vostros, and we never did. We make our living from repairs, upgrades and monthly support — and when a machine genuinely is past it, we can supply a refurbished business-grade Dell from stock or order a new Dell to spec through our reseller account. We earn either way, so we’ve no reason to steer you wrong in either direction. If a modest repair gets three more years from a Vostro 5510, we’ll say so. If you’re about to spend £200 propping up a machine that can’t run Windows 11, we’ll say that too.

For businesses, the bigger question is usually the fleet, not the machine — and that’s covered further down this page.

Common faults

The Vostro problems we fix most often

These are the failures Dorset’s Vostro owners actually bring us — each one documented by Dell’s own support pages or long-running Dell Community threads, not invented for a web page. Every job starts with a proper diagnosis, and you get a quoted range before we touch anything.

The touchpad or keyboard is lifting up

Stop using the machine. This is almost always a swollen battery pushing the case apart from the inside — a known age-related failure Dell covers in its own swollen-battery guidance, and one the Vostro service manuals warn about specifically. Dell’s advice is not to remove the pack yourself (most Vostro batteries are internal), and we agree: unplug it, power it down, and call us. Battery replacement normally cures both the safety issue and the deformed touchpad.

“Plugged in, not charging” — or the adapter isn’t recognised at all

A classic Vostro complaint with three usual suspects: a worn DC-in jack, a failed identification pin in the charger itself, or the charging circuitry on the board. When the machine can’t identify its adapter it may also deliberately slow itself down, so a “charging problem” often shows up first as a performance problem. Dell’s adapter troubleshooting guide covers the software checks; the physical repairs are bench work we do routinely.

Cracking around the hinge or charging-port corner

Budget-range plastics are the Vostro’s weak spot. There’s a well-known Dell Community thread from an organisation running 250+ Vostro 5568s that reported near-universal chassis and hinge failure at the charging-port corner. Caught early, a hinge repair is far cheaper than the cracked screen that follows if the lid keeps flexing. If yours is starting to creak or gape at one corner, that’s the moment to act.

Wi-Fi keeps dropping

Recent Vostros with the Realtek RTL8852BE wireless card have a Dell-documented dropout issue — Dell names Vostro specifically and prescribes a driver fix. Older models have their own known disconnect causes (power-management settings, Bluetooth interference). Often fixable remotely in one remote support session, without the machine leaving your desk.

Running hot, fan roaring — or booting to a black screen

Dust-clogged coolers and dried thermal paste make an ageing Vostro loud, hot and prone to crashes; in the worst cases the machine powers on with sounds but no display. A strip-down clean and repaste is one of the most cost-effective services we do, and it usually quietens the fan dramatically too.

Dead machine — lights blinking in a pattern

Vostros signal power and startup faults with LED blink codes — Dell publishes a dedicated Vostro diagnostic-indicators guide precisely because these callouts are so common. Note the pattern (how many amber, how many white) and tell us when you call: it helps us arrive with the right parts, or tell you honestly over the phone if it sounds like a board-level fault where repair may not be economical.

Upgrades

The upgrade that saves a slow Vostro from the skip

If your Vostro isn’t broken but takes five minutes to become usable each morning, don’t replace it yet. A huge number of Vostro 3000-series machines shipped with a slow 5400rpm mechanical hard drive and just 4GB of RAM — a combination that made them feel old within a couple of years. Dell’s own community forums are full of threads like “Vostro 14 3000 is incredibly slow”, and the consensus fix is always the same: a solid-state drive and a RAM top-up.

It’s the single best-value job in computing. We fit the SSD, move Windows, all your programs and every file across so the machine comes back exactly as you left it — just several times faster to boot, open files and switch between apps. For a small business, doing this across three or four eligible machines costs a fraction of one new computer. More detail on how we do it is on our SSD upgrade page.

Two honest caveats. First, we check your exact model before quoting — some Vostro configurations have limited RAM headroom, and we’d rather tell you that up front. Second, an SSD makes a machine faster; it doesn’t make it Windows 11-eligible. If your Vostro has an older processor that can’t officially run Windows 11, we’ll fold that into the keep-or-replace advice below rather than sell you an upgrade on borrowed time. Our guide to Windows 10 ESU versus upgrading your Dell explains the options.

Naming, explained

What happened to Vostro — and what “Dell Pro Essential” means

Short version for anyone Googling “is Dell Vostro discontinued”: yes. At CES in January 2025, Dell retired the Vostro name along with Inspiron, Latitude, XPS and Precision, moving to a three-tier scheme — Dell, Dell Pro and Dell Pro Max. Dell’s own transition page says Vostro is no longer available to buy, and that its small-business role — the sensible security features, the business-grade support options — now lives in the Dell Pro Essential tier. Existing Vostro warranties and ProSupport contracts are being honoured through to the end of their terms.

What that means in practice:

  • If you own Vostros: nothing changes for repair and support. The machines are the same hardware they were the day before the announcement, spare parts remain available, and we’ll keep servicing Vostro 3000, 5000 and 7000-series laptops and Vostro desktops for as long as they’re on desks — which, realistically, is years.
  • If you’re replacing one with new: the like-for-like successor is a Dell Pro Essential laptop or desktop, and as an independent Dell reseller we can order any current Dell line to spec — no stock pressure, just the configuration your business actually needs.
  • One thing worth knowing: Dell’s new naming has kept shifting since 2025, so if a quote you receive elsewhere uses unfamiliar model names, ask us — translating Dell’s badges is now genuinely part of the job.

Keep or replace

Fleet triage: which Vostros to keep, which to retire

If you run several Vostros, the smart move isn’t a machine-by-machine panic — it’s one honest look at the lot. We do this for Dorset small businesses as a simple triage: for each machine we weigh its age, its fault history, its upgrade headroom and — the big one right now — its Windows position. Windows 10 support ended on 14 October 2025, and the Extended Security Updates lifeline for consumers runs out on 12 October 2027. A Vostro that can’t officially run Windows 11 has a hard deadline, however healthy its hardware.

The triage usually sorts a fleet into three piles:

  • Keep and service: Windows 11-capable machines with routine faults or none. A clean, a battery, maybe an SSD — then onto a maintenance rhythm.
  • Keep for now, plan the exit: machines doing light duties that can safely see out ESU, with a replacement date in the diary rather than a surprise.
  • Replace: machines where repair cost, age and the Windows deadline all point the same way.

For replacements, we’ll always be straight about the options. We stock refurbished business-grade Dells — Latitude laptops from £510 and OptiPlex desktops from £545 (guide prices) — each fitted with a brand-new 1TB Samsung 990 PRO SSD, set up with Windows 11, your data transferred, with a 12-month return-to-base warranty (five years on a support plan). Honest caveat: a refurbished Latitude is a sturdier machine than the Vostro it replaces, but it’s not brand new — if your situation calls for new, we order a Dell Pro Essential (or any Dell line) to spec through our reseller account instead. Browse the current refurbished stock on our Dell hardware page, and if new prices surprise you, our note on why computer prices have gone up explains the market honestly. Retiring old machines? We handle secure IT disposal so your business data doesn’t walk out the door with the hardware.

Frequently asked

Is Dell Vostro discontinued?

Yes. Dell retired the Vostro name in January 2025, moving its small-business range into the new Dell Pro family — the closest successor tier is Dell Pro Essential. Existing Vostro warranties are being honoured, and the machines themselves are still perfectly serviceable. We continue to repair and support all Vostro laptops and desktops across Dorset.

What replaced the Dell Vostro?

Dell Pro Essential laptops and desktops carry the old Vostro positioning — sensible business machines without the premium price. As an independent Dell reseller since 2001 we can order Dell Pro Essential (or any current Dell line) to your spec. We also stock refurbished business-grade Latitude laptops from £510 and OptiPlex desktops from £545 as a lower-cost alternative.

Is my Dell Vostro worth repairing?

Usually, yes — the common faults (battery, charging port, hinge, cooling, slow hard drive) are routine and economical to fix. The main exception is a machine that cannot officially run Windows 11, because Windows 10 support ended on 14 October 2025 and the ESU extension runs out on 12 October 2027. We diagnose first and tell you straight either way; we do not stock Vostros, so there is no sales agenda behind the advice.

My Vostro’s touchpad is lifting up — what’s wrong?

That is the classic sign of a swollen battery pushing the case apart from inside, a failure Dell documents in its own guidance. Stop using the machine, unplug it and do not try to remove the battery yourself — most Vostro packs are internal. We collect the machine free locally and a battery replacement normally puts everything right.

Why does my Vostro say “plugged in, not charging”?

Three usual causes: a worn DC-in jack, a failed identification pin in the charger, or a fault in the board’s charging circuit. When the laptop cannot identify its adapter it may also deliberately run slower, so charging faults often masquerade as performance problems. We diagnose which it is before quoting anything.

Can you fit an SSD in a Vostro 3520 or other 3000-series model?

Yes — the SSD-plus-RAM upgrade is the single best-value fix for the many Vostro 3000-series machines that shipped with slow mechanical drives and 4GB of RAM. We move Windows, your programs and all your data across, so it comes back working exactly as before, just far faster. We check your exact model’s upgrade headroom before quoting.

Why does my Vostro’s Wi-Fi keep dropping?

Recent Vostros with the Realtek RTL8852BE wireless card have a Dell-documented dropout issue with a prescribed driver fix; older models have their own known causes such as power-management settings. This is often fixable in a single remote support session without the machine leaving your desk.

Do you support small businesses running a fleet of Vostros?

Yes — that is our core work. Business support plans start from £24.38 per month per computer and include a full service of every covered machine every six weeks with a written Service Report left on the Desktop. We also run a keep-or-replace triage across a fleet so you can budget replacements calmly instead of reacting to failures.

Do you have a shop I can bring my Vostro to?

No — we have no walk-in shop, and honestly think you get a better service without one. We collect faulty machines free across Bournemouth, Poole, Christchurch and the wider Dorset area, visit businesses on site, fix many faults remotely, and can meet by appointment at Kinson Community Centre. Call 01202 775566 or text 07520 615332 to arrange it.

Can my Vostro still be used safely now Windows 10 support has ended?

Windows 10 support ended on 14 October 2025; paid Extended Security Updates keep patches flowing until 12 October 2027. If your Vostro can officially run Windows 11 we can upgrade it; if it cannot, ESU buys planning time but not a future. Our Windows 10 ESU-or-upgrade guide walks through the honest options for each machine.

One Vostro playing up — or a whole fleet to think about?

Tell us the symptoms and how many machines you’re running. We’ll diagnose first, quote honestly, and never push a replacement you don’t need. Mon–Fri 9–5, call 01202 775566 or text 07520 615332.
