// FIRST FIVE MINUTES

Dell just died? Here’s what to do first

When a Dell suddenly won’t start, the temptation is to keep jabbing the power button — but a calm 60 seconds first often saves the day. A couple of safe checks anyone can do:

  • Try a power drain. Unplug the charger, take out the battery if it’s a removable-battery Dell, hold the power button for 30 seconds, then plug back in and try again.
  • Rule out the power supply. A different wall socket, a firmly seated charger, and the charger light actually being on tell you whether it’s a dead charger rather than a dead laptop.
  • Note the symptoms. Any beeps, a blinking Caps Lock light (a known Dell no-boot clue), or a specific error on screen — jot it down. It tells us a lot before we even connect.

You can keep troubleshooting from here — but if it still won’t boot, or you’re staring at a blue screen or a “no boot device” message, that’s the moment to stop and call. Guessing with an unstable Dell risks losing data you could otherwise keep. Ring us on 01202 775566 and we’ll tell you straight away whether it’s a quick remote fix or a hardware job.

// SAME-DAY RESPONSE

Same-day response and remote diagnosis, often within the hour

Our fastest route into an emergency is an SOS remote session. During opening hours (Mon—Fri, 9 to 5) we can usually be looking at your Dell within the hour — no waiting for an appointment, no driving anywhere. You run a small tool, read us a code, and we connect over Splashtop.

  • We watch, you stay in control. We phone before we connect, you see everything on your screen, and the session ends the moment we’re done.
  • Ideal for software emergencies. Windows won’t load past the login, a driver update has broken something, email has fallen over, alarming pop-ups, a botched Windows update — these are often fixed remotely the same day.
  • Honest about limits. If the Dell is completely dead or has a hardware fault, remote can’t reach it — we switch straight to collection rather than waste your time.

A quick word on what “emergency” means with us: we offer same-day response and same-day remote diagnosis where possible — we are not a 24/7 line, and we never promise a guaranteed same-day physical repair, because that depends on the parts your Dell needs. We’ll always be straight about timing. Where a fault is common and we have the part, we move fast — but a physical repair can only be as quick as the part it needs, so we quote you honestly rather than promise a same-day miracle. // STRAIGHT TALK

What we can rush — and what we can’t

We’d rather set honest expectations than over-promise in a panic. Here’s the plain version:

  • We can rush the response. Same-day contact, and remote diagnosis often within the hour while we’re open (Mon—Fri, 9 to 5). We are not a 24/7 service.
  • We can’t guarantee a same-day physical repair. If your Dell needs a screen, battery or drive we don’t have on the shelf, the timeline depends on the part — and we’ll tell you exactly where you stand.
  • We’re an independent Dell specialist. Thirty years working with Dell laptops and desktops, but we are not a Dell Authorised Service Provider and this is not official Dell warranty repair or Dell ProSupport.
  • Dell means Windows. We look after Windows Dell laptops and desktops (and Android phones) — not Apple or Mac.

None of that slows down the important bit: picking up the phone and getting eyes on your problem fast. Start with an emergency IT help call and we’ll take it from there.

// GOOD QUESTIONS

Frequently asked

Can you fix my Dell the same day in Bournemouth?

We give a same-day response and, where possible, same-day remote diagnosis — across Bournemouth we can often be looking at your Dell over an SOS session within the hour during opening hours (Mon—Fri, 9 to 5). We can’t promise a guaranteed same-day physical repair, because that depends on the parts your Dell needs, but we’ll give you the realistic timeline straight away.

My Dell won’t turn on at all — can you help remotely?

If it’s completely dead, remote support can’t reach it. Try a quick power drain first (unplug, hold the power button for 30 seconds, plug back in); if it still won’t boot, it’s a hardware job. We’ll arrange free local collection or you can drop it in, and we diagnose it under our no-fix-no-fee promise.

How quickly can you look at my Dell remotely?

During opening hours (Mon—Fri, 9 to 5) we can usually start an SOS remote session within the hour. We phone before we connect, you watch the whole time, and the session ends as soon as we’re finished. Software emergencies — a stuck Windows update, a broken driver, email failures — are often sorted the same day this way.

Is there a call-out or emergency fee?

No. There’s no call-out fee and free local collection across Bournemouth, and our diagnosis is no-fix-no-fee — if we can’t help, you don’t pay for the look. Repairs come with a 12-month warranty on the work we do.

Are you Dell’s official emergency repair service?

No — we’re an independent Dell specialist with 30 years’ experience on Dell laptops and desktops. We are not a Dell Authorised Service Provider, and this is not official Dell warranty repair or Dell ProSupport. That independence is often why we can respond faster and without the runaround.

Our office Dells are down — can you prioritise a business?

Yes. We prioritise business emergencies in Bournemouth and across Dorset with a same-day response and remote triage first to get you working where we can.
