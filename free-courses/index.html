// GOOD QUESTIONS

Frequently asked

Are these courses really free?

Completely — no sign-up, no card details, nothing to install, and no catch. They run in your web browser. We’re a family IT firm, and helping people get confident with technology is genuinely good for everyone (and yes, some people become customers — that’s the whole business model, out in the open).

Who are they for?

Anyone, but they’re written especially for beginners and older learners — plain English, short lessons, no time pressure, no pass marks, and nothing to be embarrassed about. Thousands of people locally are in exactly the same boat.

Do I get a certificate?

Yes — every course ends with a ‘365 Techies Certificate of Completion’ you can pop your name on and print or keep. It’s our own friendly certificate rather than an accredited qualification — and each course also gives you a printable one-page checklist to keep by the computer.

Will you save my progress?

Your progress is remembered by your own web browser on your own computer — nothing you do or type is stored by us or sent anywhere. Come back any time and pick up where you left off (on the same device).

Can I learn in person instead?

Yes! We run patient, friendly computer lessons at the Kinson Community Centre in Bournemouth, and one-to-one help too. Ask about lessons or call 01202 775566.

Prefer a friendly human and a cup of tea?

Our in-person computer lessons at the Kinson Community Centre are patient, plain-English and genuinely fun. Or ring us — no silly questions, ever.
