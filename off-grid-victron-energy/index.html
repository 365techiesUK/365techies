💡 Golden rules (read before you build)

  • Fuse every positive as close to the battery as practical — the fuse is there to protect the cable, not the gadget.
  • Fat and short beats long and thin. Keep inverter cables as short as possible, and if you parallel batteries, keep the pair’s leads equal length.
  • Crimp, don’t solder. Soldered joints crack with vehicle vibration; a properly crimped lug with adhesive heatshrink lasts decades.
  • Put a battery isolator switch on the main positive — one twist to make the whole system safe to work on.
  • Everything negative goes through the shunt. One cable from the battery negative to the shunt, and every load’s negative on the far side — or your state-of-charge reading is fiction.
  • Torque terminals to the manual’s spec and re-check them after your first weekend away — connections bed in.
  • Ventilate and protect from temperature. Lithium won’t charge below 0°C (the SuperPack protects itself) — inside the living space beats an unheated garage box.
  • Know where DIY ends. 12V is friendly; the 230V side (hook-up, sockets, RCDs) is qualified-electrician territory, and gas is Gas Safe territory. No exceptions.

// IN DETAIL

Power built for how you live & work

  • Off-grid & backup power for homes

    Whole-home off-grid living, or seamless backup when the grid fails — solar, a lithium battery bank and a Victron inverter/charger that switches over in milliseconds. Run the essentials (or everything) on your own stored, clean energy, and see it all on VRM.

  • Energy resilience for businesses

    Keep tills, broadband, servers and fridges running through power cuts, and trim bills by storing solar or off-peak energy for busy times. We size resilient Victron systems to your real load, with remote monitoring so problems are caught before they cost you.

  • Campervans — built for van life

    Proper 12V systems for weekend trips or full-time travel — roof solar, a lithium leisure battery, a Victron MPPT and inverter, and DC-DC charging that tops up from the engine as you drive. Silent power for the fridge, lights, devices and induction hob, watched from your phone.

  • Motorhomes & overlanding

    Dependable power for everything you run on the road — larger battery banks, inverters for 230V appliances, solar and smart charging, all built on Victron and set up so a screen (or the VRM app) shows charge, solar and usage at a glance.

  • Boats & marine

    Reliable, well-monitored power afloat — 12/24V lithium banks, inverter/chargers for shore power and underway, solar and marine-grade battery monitoring. Always know your state of charge, so you’re never caught out away from the pontoon.

  • Garden offices, cabins & sites

    Power where there’s no easy mains run — studios, home offices, shepherd’s huts, glamping and remote sites. Solar, storage and inverters sized to the job, with remote monitoring so you can keep an eye from afar.
