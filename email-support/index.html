// GOOD QUESTIONS

Frequently asked

Why is my email not sending or receiving?

There are several common causes — settings, passwords, full mailboxes or server issues. We diagnose it remotely and get your email flowing again.

Can you move my old email to Microsoft 365?

Yes — we migrate email, contacts and calendars to Microsoft 365 with minimal disruption.

Can you check if an email is safe?

Yes — subscribers can always ask us to check a suspicious message before clicking anything.

Why is my Outlook not receiving emails?

Common causes are a full mailbox, wrong settings, a changed password, Outlook stuck ‘working offline’, or a rule filing mail elsewhere. We’ll diagnose it remotely and get Outlook receiving again.

How do I recover a hacked email account?

Act fast — change the password, turn on two-factor authentication and check for sneaky forwarding rules. We can take it from there: secure the account, lock the intruder out and check nothing else was touched. See cybersecurity.

My email stopped working after changing broadband or provider — can you fix it?

Yes — it’s one of our most common jobs. Whether your business email died because the domain expired, you need to move Plusnet email to Gmail before it closes, or your BT email won’t add to the new Outlook — we sort it remotely, usually the same day.
