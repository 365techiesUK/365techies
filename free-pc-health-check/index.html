// EASY TO MIX UP

Is this Microsoft’s “PC Health Check” or “PC Manager”?

No — similar names, different things. Microsoft’s PC Health Check mainly answers one question: can this PC run Windows 11? Microsoft’s PC Manager clean-up utility isn’t officially available to download in the UK — it has been region-limited out of the UK Microsoft Store for years (as of mid-2026). 365 PC Manager is different: made in Dorset for UK users, a plain-English health check of the things that actually catch people out — drive health, backup, protection, age and value — your data stays on your PC, and a real local techie is one tap away when you want a human.

// GOOD QUESTIONS

Frequently asked

Is the 365 PC Manager app really free?

Yes — the health score, live performance graphs, one-tap boost, startup and storage tools are free forever, no sign-up. If you go on a 365 support plan we unlock the extra bits (priority help, backup checks and more), but you never have to.

Will it slow my PC down?

No. It’s tiny and sits quietly in your system tray, only doing anything when you open it or ask it to. The live graphs run only while you’re watching them.

Is it safe to install?

Completely. It only reads your PC’s health (memory, disk, antivirus, backup) and never changes anything without you tapping a button. It’s made by 365 Techies, a family-run Dorset IT firm here since 1995, rated 4.9 on Google.

Does it send my information anywhere?

If you’re a 365 customer and we’ve given you a key, it sends us your PC’s health score so we can spot problems early and help proactively — nothing personal, just the health check. Not a customer? It keeps everything on your PC.

What does ‘Boost’ actually do?

It safely clears the temporary-file clutter Windows leaves behind and refreshes memory — a quick pick-me-up when your PC feels sluggish. It never touches your documents, photos or programs.

When does it launch, and how do I get it?

It’s in final testing now. Join the waitlist and we’ll email you the download the moment it’s ready — and if you’d rather not install it yourself, we’ll happily set it up for you free, by hand, when it’s ready. We won’t promise a date until it’s genuinely ready; we’d rather ship it right than rush it.

Why can’t I download Microsoft’s PC Manager in the UK?

Microsoft hasn’t made its “PC Manager” utility officially available in the UK Microsoft Store — it has been region-limited for years (still, as of mid-2026). If what you actually wanted was a friendly Windows health check and tidy-up from someone you can phone, that’s exactly what our free 365 PC Manager is: made in Dorset, works right here, and your data stays on your PC.
