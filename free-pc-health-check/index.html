The most thorough PC service we can find in Dorset

Every six weeks, on a support plan, your computer gets the service a business would expect: every update applied, every security setting checked, every drive and backup verified — and the whole thing written up. The Service Report is emailed to you and kept in your portal for good: a dated record that your computer was kept safe, secure and up to date.

One standard, home or business

Businesses have always had this level of care. We can’t find another company in Dorset that gives home customers exactly the same service, the same checks and the same written report. One laptop in a spare room or twenty in an office: the work and the write-up are identical.

Built on more than thirty years of doing it

This is the routine we have refined over three decades of servicing and supplying computers in Bournemouth, as a family firm and a Microsoft Partner. Every item in it is there because we have seen what happens when it is missed.

What every service covers

  • Windows updates

    Installed and verified — including the ones that fail quietly in the background.

  • Driver updates

    Chipset, graphics, network and the maker’s own drivers — the ones Windows Update leaves behind.

  • Application updates

    Browsers, Office, Zoom, Reader and the rest. An old app with a known hole is how most home PCs get caught.

  • Security check

    Antivirus state, firewall, drive encryption and a scan — each read on the day and written down as found.

  • System file check

    Windows verified against itself and repaired where it can be, so faults are caught before they cost you a morning.

  • Backup checked

    Whether last night’s backup actually ran, and how much room is left before the drive quietly stops protecting you.

  • Broadband speed test

    Measured from your machine and compared with your last visit, so a line that has slipped gets noticed by us first.

  • Drive health & tune-up

    Hours run, temperature, data written and wear on every drive — plus temporary files cleared and drives optimised.

Written up, every time

You do not have to take our word for it. The report shows your score and how it moved, the security position on the day, what was done, and the state of every drive and your backup — emailed to you, kept in your portal, and left on your Desktop.

It tests your broadband too

Most PC tools stop at the PC. 365 PC Manager also measures your actual broadband speed — download, upload and latency — and remembers it, so slow internet stops being an argument and starts being a number.

  • It knows Wi‑Fi from your line

    A Wi‑Fi reading is the speed at your desk, not the speed your provider sells you. The app says so plainly, so you never complain to your provider about a Wi‑Fi problem.

// GOOD QUESTIONS

Frequently asked

Is the 365 PC Manager app really free?

Yes — the health score, live performance graphs, one-tap boost, startup and storage tools are free forever, no sign-up. If you go on a 365 support plan we unlock the extra bits (priority help, backup checks and more), but you never have to.

What is the written Service Report?

After every six-weekly service on a support plan we write up what we found and what we did: your computer’s health score and how it moved since last time, the security position on the day (Windows updates, firewall, antivirus and drive encryption, each read rather than assumed), every drive’s hours, temperature and wear, whether your backup ran and how much room is left, and how long any guarantee has to run. It is emailed to you, kept in your portal for good, and left on your Desktop.

How often is my computer serviced on a plan?

About every six weeks. Each service applies Windows, driver and application updates, runs a security check and a system file check, checks your backup and every drive, measures your broadband speed from the machine, and clears out temporary files — the same routine for a home laptop as for a business fleet.

Can I show the Service Report to my bank or insurer?

Yes. Each report is dated and kept on file, so if a bank or insurer asks whether your computer was protected and up to date, you can show what was checked, what was updated and when — a record of the reasonable care you took. It is the same kind of write-up we produce for a customer’s bank after a scam, only here it exists before anything goes wrong. It is evidence of care, not a guarantee of any outcome.

Will it slow my PC down?

No. It’s tiny and sits quietly in your system tray. In the background it does three small things: checks your health score about once an hour, watches the clock for any service reminder you’ve set, and quietly updates itself when a new version is out (only ever while the window is closed). None of that is anything you’d feel. The live performance graphs run only while you’re watching them.

Is it safe to install?

Yes. It reads your PC’s health (memory, disk, antivirus, backup) and changes nothing unless you tap a button. The one exception is entirely your choice: support-plan customers can switch on “let 365 run safe maintenance”, which lets us run a short fixed list of harmless tidy-up jobs — clearing temporary files, flushing the network cache, collecting a diagnostics summary — without ringing you first. It’s off unless you turn it on, and you can turn it off again any time. We’re 365 Techies, a family-run Dorset IT firm here since 1995, rated 4.9 on Google.

Does it send my information anywhere?

Yes, a little — here’s exactly what. Once an hour it contacts us with your PC’s health score and the basics behind it (whether antivirus and backup are on, how full the disk is, battery health, the PC name you chose and an anonymous ID for the machine). That is how every copy stays up to date, and how we spot a problem on a customer’s machine before it bites. It never sends your files, photos, emails, browsing or passwords. If you’re on a plan we tie it to your account so we can help proactively; if you’re not, it isn’t linked to a customer record. Run the broadband test and your connection is checked against an outside service to name your provider — see our privacy policy.

What does ‘Boost’ actually do?

It safely clears the temporary-file clutter Windows leaves behind and refreshes memory — a quick pick-me-up when your PC feels sluggish. It never touches your documents, photos or programs.

How do I get it, and is the download safe?

It’s live: tap Download free for Windows, open it, and it installs in a couple of clicks — then it lives in your Start menu like any other program, and you can remove it any time from Settings > Apps. The download is digitally signed by 365 Techies Ltd (a Microsoft-issued certificate), so Windows shows our name as the publisher rather than “unknown”. If you’d rather we set it up for you, ring 01202 775566 and we’ll do it free, by hand.

Why can’t I download Microsoft’s PC Manager in the UK?

Microsoft hasn’t made its “PC Manager” utility officially available in the UK Microsoft Store — it has been region-limited for years (still, as of mid-2026). If what you actually wanted was a friendly Windows health check and tidy-up from someone you can phone, that’s exactly what our free 365 PC Manager is: made in Dorset, works right here, and your data stays on your PC.
